NALVAI (DEMO)

Nalvai's Assortment of Lojban Vocabulary Attestation Instances

These are the words I intend to include in the NALVAI project. This list is largely based on a frequency list, although any words used by the community is welcome. Suggest a word to be included!

Currently the words here links to their corresponding entries in jbovlaste. Visit the main page to see their entry in NALVAI!

In this word list, a word with a fully filled background means that it currently has an entry in NALVAI. A word with only a border means that it has not yet been included, although I intend to include it in the future.
