
-entry:
    -word to
    -gloss Text Structure: Open parenthesis
    -type cmavo
    -contributor la ninlat.
    -definition:
        -selmaho TO
        -meaning:
            -exp Opens a parenthetical note. The text inside must be grammatical Lojban. Terminate with {{toi}}.
            -note The text inside contains additional information that isn't essential to the meaning of the sentence, but can still help. 
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi djuno ri'a lo su'u mi cmima le jatna kamni pe la logbau skejgu {{to la'o gy ~ The Loglan Institute ~ gy toi}}
                -english I know, because I'm a member of the leader comitee associated with the science institute of Loglan {{([English] ~ The Loglan Institute ~)}}
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la janbe
            -example:
                -lojban le mi bruna {{to se rirni le se rirni pe le mamta pe le mi mamta toi}} cu bilma
                -english My brother, {{(my mother's mother's child's child)}} is ill.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la kanis
                -note Note that the {{toi}} in this sentence is elidable, as the content in the parentheses cannot take the second selbri introduced by {{cu}}.
            -example:
                -lojban .i da'i {{to se va'u lo nu darlu toi}} lo jmive je pensi cu kakne lo nu zasti ze'o lo bitmu be lo botpi
                -english ..., granting {{(for the sake of argument)}} that life and thought exist beyond the walls of the caraffe.
                -source-id cloc
                -note If the {{toi}} was elided in this sentence, the parentheses will automatically close just before the {{cu}}, for the same reason as in the previous example.

-entry:
    -word va'o
    -gloss Preposition: under conditions ... / in environment ...
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho BAI
        -meaning:
            -exp Under conditions ... / In environment ... ; Introduces an unnumbered sumti that plays the role of {{vanbi}}'s x<sub>1</sub> in the sentence.
            -note The event described by the bridi occurs under the conditions described by the tagged sumti.
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo nu badri cu norxla {{va'o lo nu do na sisti lo ka troci}}
                -english It's okay to feel sad {{as long as you don't give up}}.
                -source-id clan
            -example:
                -lojban .i xu da do prali lo nu sanga le do zgike {{va'o lo nu le tertigni na se vajni co tinju'i do}}
                -english Would it do you any good to sing your heart out {{if the audience didn't care to listen to you}}?
                -source-id nabm
        -meaning:
            -context {{se}} ~
            -exp As condition/environment for ...; Introduces an unnumbered sumti that plays the role of {{vanbi}}'s x<sub>2</sub> in the sentence.
            -note The event described by the bridi is the condition/environment under which the referent of the tagged sumti occurs. 
            -example:
                -lojban .i ku'i {{se va'o mi}} ba da lo kandi manku ca'o culno 
                -english But darkness had already been gathering {{round me}}.
                -source-id inag

-entry:
    -word xm
    -gloss Attitudinal: "hmm", thoughtful humming
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho Y
        -meaning:
            -exp Express pondering; thoughtful humming noise. Equivalent to English "Hmm".
            -note Although morphologically a cmevla and is to be converted to an attitudinal with {{ki'ai}}, <a href="https://discord.com/channels/771781383361921024/771782606932803606/779777415232487424">vowelless cmevla are declared to function as free modifiers in a 2020 roljbogu'e message</a> and such rule is adopted by some. Equivalent to {{xy'y}}, though {{xm}} is much more common. Alternative forms include {{xmm}} and {{xmmm}}.
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{xm}} si'au lo ka djuno fi lo lojyske ja'a plixau va'o po flalu zukte
                -english {{Hmm,}} seems that knowing logic is indeed useful when doing legal stuffs.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker Lucilla
                -note This example is in jboponei.
            -example:
                -lojban {{xm}} mi toltugni i lo si'o cnitsa zo'u lu ge'esai li'u matmau
                -english {{Hmm}} I disagree, for [expressing] strong emotions "ge'esai" is more appropriate. 
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la uakci
            -example:
                -lojban {{xmm}} simlu loka mi na ka'e kansa
                -english {{Hmm}} seems that I cannot be with [you] 
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la ziren

-entry:
    -word za'a
    -gloss Attitudinal (evidential): I directly observe
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho UI2
        -meaning:
            -exp Evidential: used to express direct observation or perception as the source of information or idea.
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo nu kansa cu se nitcu .ie .i ku'i {{za'a}} do na djica
                -english Togetherness is needed. But, {{I observe}}, you don't want that.
                -source-id nafe
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi ca {{mabla za'a}} ciska fo lo batkyfoi
                -english {{I see}} I'm typing {{badly}} with my keyboard now.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la srasu
                -note The {{za'a}} applies directly to {{mabla}}, which emphasizes that it is the badness (of typing) that is observed.
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{za'a dai}} mi jai katna lo plise lo re mei
                -english {{See}}, I will cut the apple in half.
                -source-id snow

-entry:
    -word ze'e
    -gloss Preposition (tense): mark unbounded time interval
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho ZEhA
        -meaning:
            -exp Unbounded duration: signifies that the event lasts for an unlimited time.
            -note Although the CLL defines {{ze'e}} as "infinite duration", meaning the event extends infinitely into the past and/or future, actual usage is less strict. It only requires that the event span "as long as applicable". See the usage examples of {{ze'e pu}} and {{ze'e ba}} below.
            -example:
                -lojban .i ca {{ze'e ru'i}} tcika fa li xa
                -english It's {{always}} six o'clock now.
                -source-id alic
        -meaning:
            -context ~ {{pu}}
            -exp signifies that the event extends unboundedly to the past.
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o pa mai la ckuzda cu {{ze'e pu}} zasti
                -english First: The Library has existed {{ab æternitate}}. 
                -source-id libr
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo po'o cmana poi {{ze'e pu slabu ny}} cu me lo ci je'erma'a noi galtu dunli lo ny cidni
                -english The only mountains {{he had ever known}} were the three volcanoes, which came up to his knees. 
                -source-id litt
        -meaning:
            -context ~ {{ba}}
            -exp signifies that the event extends unboundedly to the future.
            -example:
                -lojban .i je va'o lo nu do bevri cy mi'a zo'u mi'a do {{ze'e ba}} tai ckire zei mutce
                -english If you will bring him back to us, we shall thank you {{ever and ever}} so much.
                -source-id wond
            -example:
                -lojban .i do mi {{ze'e ba}} pendo
                -english You {{will always}} be my friend.
                -source-id litt
        -meaning:
            -context When followed by a sumti
            -variation CLL, BPFK
            -exp mark the following sumti as the unbounded time duration of the event.
            -note Although specified by <em>both</em> the CLL and the BPFK, this usage is unattested. See below.
        -meaning:
            -context When followed by a sumti
            -exp During the entirety of ...
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{ze'e lo nunji'e be mi}} zo'u mi djica lo nu stuvi'e gy.
                -english I {{always}} wanted to visit it {{in my whole life}}.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker qimar


-entry:
    -word zoi
    -gloss Quotation: start non-Lojban quotation
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho ZOI
        -meaning:
            -exp Begin non-Lojban quotation. The next Lojban word loses all its grammatical function, becomes the quote delimiter, and quotes all further text until the same word is repeated. The quote delimiters and the quoted text must be separated with pauses. The entire construct (from {{zoi}} to the second delimiter) is a sumti meaning "the text &lt;quoted text&gt;", and is considered a single word for grammatical purposes.
            -note The delimiter is usually chosen to give hints to the nature of the quoted text. For example, the first letter of the source language, or {{urli}} when it is an URL. {{zoi}} is often used as the default delimiter if no hints is to be given (in which case the construct is {{zoi zoi. &lt;quoted text&gt; .zoi}}).
            -example:
                -lojban .i ku'i la mapypre jo'u la cibmasti cicyractu cu bacru {{zoi sance c c sance}}
                -english ..., but the Hatter and the March Hare went {{"Sh! sh!"}}
                -source-id alic
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{zoi .urli. blinry.org/toki-pona-cheat-sheet/ .urli.}} ti urli
                -english Link for this: {{blinry.org/toki-pona-cheat-sheet/}}
                -source-id tokp
            -example:
                -lojban .i mu'i gi so'o da te smuni lu kampu bangu li'u {{la'e zoi gy. lingua franca .gy}} gi mi te smuni zo kampu tu'a lo nu le bangu cu jai se pi'o kakne fai lo vrici be tu'a lo jbebau lo ka casnu
                -english Based on the use of the phrase "vehicular language" by some as a synonym for {{lingua franca}}, we use the term vehicular to refer to the extent to which a language is used to facilitate communication among those who speak different first languages.
                -source-id asse
                -note The quote delimiter is chosen to be {{gy.}} because the quoted text, "lingua franca", is in English ({{<u>g</u>libau}}). The {{la'e}} is there to turn the sumti's meaning from "the phrase 'lingua franca'" to "the meaning of the phrase 'lingua franca'", making the sumti fit the x<sub>1</sub> of {{smuni}}.
