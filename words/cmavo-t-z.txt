
-entry:
    -word to
    -gloss Text Structure: Open parenthesis
    -type cmavo
    -contributor la ninlat.
    -definition:
        -selmaho TO
        -meaning:
            -exp Opens a parenthetical note. The text inside must be grammatical Lojban. Terminate with {{toi}}.
            -note The text inside contains additional information that isn't essential to the meaning of the sentence, but can still help. 
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi djuno ri'a lo su'u mi cmima le jatna kamni pe la logbau skejgu {{to la'o gy ~ The Loglan Institute ~ gy toi}}
                -english I know, because I'm a member of the leader comitee associated with the science institute of Loglan {{([English] ~ The Loglan Institute ~)}}
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la janbe
            -example:
                -lojban le mi bruna {{to se rirni le se rirni pe le mamta pe le mi mamta toi}} cu bilma
                -english My brother, {{(my mother's mother's child's child)}} is ill.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la kanis
                -note Note that the {{toi}} in this sentence is elidable, as the content in the parentheses cannot take the second selbri introduced by {{cu}}.
            -example:
                -lojban .i da'i {{to se va'u lo nu darlu toi}} lo jmive je pensi cu kakne lo nu zasti ze'o lo bitmu be lo botpi
                -english ..., granting {{(for the sake of argument)}} that life and thought exist beyond the walls of the caraffe.
                -source-id cloc
                -note If the {{toi}} was elided in this sentence, the parentheses will automatically close just before the {{cu}}, for the same reason as in the previous example.

-entry:
    -word va'o
    -gloss Preposition: under conditions ... / in environment ...
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho BAI
        -meaning:
            -exp Under conditions ... / In environment ... ; Introduces an unnumbered sumti that plays the role of {{vanbi}}'s x<sub>1</sub> in the sentence.
            -note The event described by the bridi occurs under the conditions described by the tagged sumti.
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo nu badri cu norxla {{va'o lo nu do na sisti lo ka troci}}
                -english It's okay to feel sad {{as long as you don't give up}}.
                -source-id clan
            -example:
                -lojban .i xu da do prali lo nu sanga le do zgike {{va'o lo nu le tertigni na se vajni co tinju'i do}}
                -english Would it do you any good to sing your heart out {{if the audience didn't care to listen to you}}?
                -source-id nabm
        -meaning:
            -context {{se}} ~
            -exp As condition/environment for ...; Introduces an unnumbered sumti that plays the role of {{vanbi}}'s x<sub>2</sub> in the sentence.
            -note The event described by the bridi is the condition/environment under which the referent of the tagged sumti occurs. 
            -example:
                -lojban .i ku'i {{se va'o mi}} ba da lo kandi manku ca'o culno 
                -english But darkness had already been gathering {{round me}}.
                -source-id inag

-entry:
    -word ze'e
    -gloss Preposition (tense): mark unbounded time interval
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho ZEhA
        -meaning:
            -exp Unbounded duration: signifies that the event lasts for an unlimited time.
            -note Although the CLL defines {{ze'e}} as "infinite duration", meaning the event extends infinitely into the past and/or future, actual usage is less strict. It only requires that the event span "as long as applicable". See the usage examples of {{ze'e pu}} and {{ze'e ba}} below.
            -example:
                -lojban .i ca {{ze'e ru'i}} tcika fa li xa
                -english It's {{always}} six o'clock now.
                -source-id alic
        -meaning:
            -context ~ {{pu}}
            -exp signifies that the event extends unboundedly to the past.
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o pa mai la ckuzda cu {{ze'e pu}} zasti
                -english First: The Library has existed {{ab æternitate}}. 
                -source-id libr
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo po'o cmana poi {{ze'e pu slabu ny}} cu me lo ci je'erma'a noi galtu dunli lo ny cidni
                -english The only mountains {{he had ever known}} were the three volcanoes, which came up to his knees. 
                -source-id litt
        -meaning:
            -context ~ {{ba}}
            -exp signifies that the event extends unboundedly to the future.
            -example:
                -lojban .i je va'o lo nu do bevri cy mi'a zo'u mi'a do {{ze'e ba}} tai ckire zei mutce
                -english If you will bring him back to us, we shall thank you {{ever and ever}} so much.
                -source-id wond
            -example:
                -lojban .i do mi {{ze'e ba}} pendo
                -english You {{will always}} be my friend.
                -source-id litt
        -meaning:
            -context When followed by a sumti
            -variation CLL, BPFK
            -exp mark the following sumti as the unbounded time duration of the event.
            -note Although specified by <em>both</em> the CLL and the BPFK, this usage is unattested. See below.
        -meaning:
            -context When followed by a sumti
            -exp During the entirety of ...
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{ze'e lo nunji'e be mi}} zo'u mi djica lo nu stuvi'e gy.
                -english I {{always}} wanted to visit it {{in my whole life}}.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker qimar
