
-entry:
    -word ta'i
    -gloss Preposition: by method ...
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho BAI
        -meaning:
            -exp By method ...; Introduces an unnumbered sumti that plays the role of {{tadji}}'s x_1 in the sentence.
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo se viska cu se finti {{ta'i lo nu cunso zbasu}}
                -english Here are the results {{of random generation}}.
                -source-id layo
            -example:
                -lojban .i ku'i {{ta'i ma}} mi ba skuna ti
                -english But {{how}} am I supposed to say this?
                -source-id koid
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo remna cu xaksu lo ctona'a be li so'i {{ta'i lo lijda jo'u lo marde jo'u lo pemci jo'u lo drata nalsa'u}}
                -english Man lost centuries {{over religion, morals, poetry and other fudge}};
                -source-id scie
        -meaning:
            -context {{se}} ~
            -exp As a method for doing ...; Introduces an unnumbered sumti that plays the role of {{tadji}}'s x_2 in the sentence.
            -example:
                -lojban .i ma valsi lo jai gerna be {{seta'i lonu bixygau lo gismu ke karsna fanmo ku lo drata}}
                -english What's the word for the grammar rule {{for changing the final vowel of a gismu to another [vowel]}}?
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la melmi
                -noshownote The original has {{karsnu}} instead of {{karsna}}, which I take as a typo.
        -meaning:
            -context {{te}} ~
            -exp Methodically doing (something) under conditions ...; Introduces an unnumbered sumti that plays the role of {{tadji}}'s x_3 in the sentence.
            -note Unattested.

            
-entry:
    -word to
    -gloss Text Structure: Open parenthesis
    -type cmavo
    -contributor la ninlat.
    -definition:
        -selmaho TO
        -meaning:
            -exp Opens a parenthetical note. The text inside must be grammatical Lojban. Terminate with {{toi}}.
            -note The text inside contains additional information that isn't essential to the meaning of the sentence, but can still help. 
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi djuno ri'a lo su'u mi cmima le jatna kamni pe la logbau skejgu {{to la'o gy ~ The Loglan Institute ~ gy toi}}
                -english I know, because I'm a member of the leader comitee associated with the science institute of Loglan {{([English] ~ The Loglan Institute ~)}}
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la janbe
                -contributor la ninlat.
            -example:
                -lojban le mi bruna {{to se rirni le se rirni pe le mamta pe le mi mamta toi}} cu bilma
                -english My brother, {{(my mother's mother's child's child)}} is ill.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la kanis
                -note Note that the {{toi}} in this sentence is elidable, as the content in the parentheses cannot take the second selbri introduced by {{cu}}.
                -contributor la ninlat.
            -example:
                -lojban .i da'i {{to se va'u lo nu darlu toi}} lo jmive je pensi cu kakne lo nu zasti ze'o lo bitmu be lo botpi
                -english ..., granting {{(for the sake of argument)}} that life and thought exist beyond the walls of the caraffe.
                -source-id cloc
                -note If the {{toi}} was elided in this sentence, the parentheses will automatically close just before the {{cu}}, for the same reason as in the previous example.

            -example:

-entry:
    -word ze'e
    -gloss Preposition (tense): mark unbounded time interval
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho ZEhA
        -meaning:
            -exp Unbounded duration: signifies that the event lasts for an unlimited time.
            -note Although the CLL defines {{ze'e}} as "infinite duration", meaning the event extends infinitely into the past and/or future, actual usage is less strict. It only requires that the event span "as long as applicable". See the usage examples of {{ze'e pu}} and {{ze'e ba}} below.
            -example:
                -lojban .i ca {{ze'e ru'i}} tcika fa li xa
                -english It's {{always}} six o'clock now.
                -source-id alic
        -meaning:
            -context ~ {{pu}}
            -exp signifies that the event extends unboundedly to the past.
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o pa mai la ckuzda cu {{ze'e pu}} zasti
                -english First: The Library has existed {{ab æternitate}}. 
                -source-id libr
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo po'o cmana poi {{ze'e pu slabu ny}} cu me lo ci je'erma'a noi galtu dunli lo ny cidni
                -english The only mountains {{he had ever known}} were the three volcanoes, which came up to his knees. 
                -source-id litt
        -meaning:
            -context ~ {{ba}}
            -exp signifies that the event extends unboundedly to the future.
            -example:
                -lojban .i je va'o lo nu do bevri cy mi'a zo'u mi'a do {{ze'e ba}} tai ckire zei mutce
                -english If you will bring him back to us, we shall thank you {{ever and ever}} so much.
                -source-id wond
            -example:
                -lojban .i do mi {{ze'e ba}} pendo
                -english You {{will always}} be my friend.
                -source-id litt
        -meaning:
            -context When followed by a sumti
            -variation CLL, BPFK
            -exp mark the following sumti as the unbounded time duration of the event.
            -note Although specified by <em>both</em> the CLL and the BPFK, this usage is unattested. See below.
        -meaning:
            -context When followed by a sumti
            -exp During the entirety of ...
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{ze'e lo nunji'e be mi}} zo'u mi djica lo nu stuvi'e gy.
                -english I {{always}} wanted to visit it {{in my whole life}}.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker qimar

-entry:
    -word zi
    -gloss Preposition (tense): short time distance
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho ZI
        -meaning:
            -exp Signifies the event happens / the proposition holds at some time near the reference point.
            -note {{zi}} by itself doesn't indicate the direction of the time distance. A {{pu}} or {{ba}} is commonly placed before {{zi}} to indicate the time direction. Also, {{ca zi}} is taken to mean "right at ...", as the {{zi}} is taken to shorten the interval to which {{ca}} extends to the past and/or future.
            -example:
                -lojban ko {{zi}} cliva lo zdani be mi
                -english Leave my home. {{Now!}}
                -source-id meta
            -example:
                -lojban ― coi jbope'o mi'e {{zi}} sipna <br> ― {{puzi}} ji {{bazi}} <br> ― naje li'abu'o u'i
                -english ― Hello Lojban friends, I'm sleeping {{(from a short distance in time from now)}} <br> ― {{To the past}} or {{to the future}}? <br> ― The latter, should be obvious now, haha.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la srasu, la evis
                -note The middle sentence is in JACU. Substitute {{ji}} with {{je'i}} to convert it to CLL/BPFK Lojban.
        -meaning:
            -context When followed by a sumti 
            -variation CLL
            -exp Specifies the temporal reference point to be the attached sumti. 
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o {{ba zi lo puvlamdei dormidju}} mi penmi le spesi'u
                -english {{A little past yesterday noon}} I met that couple.
                -source-id inag
        -meaning:
            -context When followed by a sumti 
            -variation BPFK
            -exp Specifies the (short) time distance to the temporal reference point to be the attached sumti.
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo prenu co'a bajra fa'a ro da gi'e jalsi'u .i ku'i bredi {{zi lo mentu be li ji'i re}} .i lo nu kelci cu cfari
                -english People began running about in all directions, tumbling up against each other; however, they got settled down {{in a minute or two}}, and the game began.
                -source-id alic
        -meaning:
            -context {{se}} ~
            -variation selba
            -exp Specifies the standard of subjective size to be the attached sumti.
            -note Under selba, a sumti after plain {{zi}} means the same as the CLL definiton. {{se zi}} is defined in selba but is never used.

-entry:
    -word zi'e
    -gloss Relative expression connector
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho ZIhE
        -meaning:
            -exp Relative expression connector. Joins relative expressions (can be either relative clauses or relative phrases), so that the relative expressions apply to the same sumti.
            -note Supplanted with {{je}} in JACU. JACU also enables other types of connection between relative expressions (for example, using {{ja}} for expressing "or" between relative expressions), which isn't possible under CLL/BPFK Lojban. 
            -example:
                -lojban .i le smani {{poi se kerlo pa da}} {{zi'e}} {{noi xendo}} cu jgari le cifnu lo birka
                -english The ape {{with the one ear}}, {{who was a good natured fellow}}, nursed the baby in his arms.
                -source-id scie
                -note In this example {{zi'e}} joins two relative clauses (one starting with {{poi}} and one starting with {{noi}}).
            -example:
                -lojban lu doi ga'i nai noltruni'u do vi mlerai<br>.i ku'i la snime blabi ku {{ne ti'a lo cmana}}<br>{{zi'e}} {{ne bu'u lo ze torcrida}}<br>cu mlemau fo lo pilji be li ki'o li'u
                -english "Queen, thou art fairest here, I hold,<br>But Snow White {{over the fells,}}<br>{{Who with the seven Dwarfs dwells,}}<br>Is fairer still a thousandfold."
                -source-id snow
                -note In this example {{zi'e}} joins two relative phrases (both starting with {{ne}}), making them both apply to the {{la snime blabi ku}}. Note that the {{ku}} here prevents the relative phrases from becoming part of the name.

-entry:
    -word zoi
    -gloss Quotation: start non-Lojban quotation
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho ZOI
        -meaning:
            -exp Begin non-Lojban quotation. The next Lojban word loses all its grammatical function, becomes the quote delimiter, and quotes all further text until the same word is repeated. The quote delimiters and the quoted text must be separated with pauses. The entire construct (from {{zoi}} to the second delimiter) is a sumti meaning "the text &lt;quoted text&gt;", and is considered a single word for grammatical purposes.
            -note The delimiter is usually chosen to give hints to the nature of the quoted text. For example, the first letter of the source language, or {{urli}} when it is an URL. {{zoi}} is often used as the default delimiter if no hints is to be given (in which case the construct is {{zoi zoi. &lt;quoted text&gt; .zoi}}).
            -example:
                -lojban .i ku'i la mapypre jo'u la cibmasti cicyractu cu bacru {{zoi sance c c sance}}
                -english ..., but the Hatter and the March Hare went {{"Sh! sh!"}}
                -source-id alic
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{zoi .urli. blinry.org/toki-pona-cheat-sheet/ .urli.}} ti urli
                -english Link for this: {{blinry.org/toki-pona-cheat-sheet/}}
                -source-id tokp
            -example:
                -lojban .i mu'i gi so'o da te smuni lu kampu bangu li'u {{la'e zoi gy. lingua franca .gy}} gi mi te smuni zo kampu tu'a lo nu le bangu cu jai se pi'o kakne fai lo vrici be tu'a lo jbebau lo ka casnu
                -english Based on the use of the phrase "vehicular language" by some as a synonym for {{lingua franca}}, we use the term vehicular to refer to the extent to which a language is used to facilitate communication among those who speak different first languages.
                -source-id asse
                -note The quote delimiter is chosen to be {{gy.}} because the quoted text, "lingua franca", is in English ({{<u>g</u>libau}}). The {{la'e}} is there to turn the sumti's meaning from "the phrase 'lingua franca'" to "the meaning of the phrase 'lingua franca'", making the sumti fit the x<sub>1</sub> of {{smuni}}.

-entry:
    -word zo'oi
    -gloss Quotation: one-word non-Lojban quotation
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho ZOhOI
        -meaning:
            -exp One-word non-Lojban quotation. The next utterance, up until its first pause (in speech) or whitespace (in writing), loses all its grammatical function and becomes the quoted content. The entire construct is a sumti meaning "the text &lt;quoted text&gt;", and is considered a single word for grammatical purposes.       
            -note Because full stops mean mandatory pauses in Lojban orthography, some consider it unacceptable to quote texts containing full stop(s) (for example, URLs) with {{zo'oi}}.     
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi na kakne lo nu xregau fi lo so'o lerfu no'u mu'a {{zo'oi dhcmrlchtdj}} zi'e poi la cevra'a ckuzda cu na pu kanpe zi'e poi bau su'o lo ri mipri bangu cu na se nenri lo betri smuni
                -english There is no combination of characters one can make &mdash; {{<i>dhcmrlchtdj</i>}}, for example &mdash; that the divine Library has not foreseen and that in one or more of its secret tongues does not hide a terrible significance. 
                -source-id libr
            -example:
                -lojban .i la gliban zo'u ji'a pagbu fa lo'e uinma'o .i {{zo'oi lol}} mupli iau u'i
                -english Attitudinals are also part of English, like {{"lol"}} lol
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la srasu
