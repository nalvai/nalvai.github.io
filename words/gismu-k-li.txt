    -gloss to be able to &mdash; (of an event) possible, (an) ability &mdash; condition of possibility/ability 
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 is able to do/be/capable of doing/being x_2 (event/state) under conditions x_3 (event/state).

                -source-id rolj

                -source-id made

                -english They reassure themselves {{as best they can}}.

-entry:

    -gloss to play, player &mdash; game, plaything, toy
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 [agent] plays with plaything/toy x_2.
            -note The meaning of "to play (a musical instrument)" is attested (most notably in <i>The Metamorphosis</i>), but is dated (last attested in 2018) and sometimes proscribed. Despite the jbovlaste definition, the x<sub>2</sub> is most commonly intepreted as "game". The x<sub>2</sub> is generally a descriptive sumti and rarely an abstraction.
            -example:
                -lojban .i ko zgana le nu le mlatu cu {{kelci}} le kelproga
                -english Watch this cat {{playing}} the video game.

                -speaker yeet kitty

                -lojban .i {{kelci}} lo nu gismu ke pixra smadi
                -english [We're] {{playing}} "guessing gismu from pictures".
                -source-id jbob
                -speaker misotanni
                -note A rare occasion of an event being in the x<sub>2</sub> of {{kelci}}. Judging from the conversation around it, the game seems to have rules of "one person draws a picture, and others try to guess what gismu it represents", and it is played on skribbl.io.
            -example:
                -lojban lo cnino tadji cu {{se kelci}} se mau lo nu nandu se troci
                -english The new method {{was}} more {{of a game}} than an effort.
                -source-id meta
