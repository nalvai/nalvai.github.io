

-entry:
    -word skami
    -gloss computer &mdash; purpose of computer
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 is a computer for purpose x_2.
            -note Both concrete and abstract are attested in the x<sub>2</sub> place.
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{lo skami}} cu kakne lo nu skicu lo gerna stura
                -english {{A computer}} can describe the grammatical structure.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la katador
            -example:
                -lojban su'o da kelci secau leka ponse le selkei je {{le ka'e skami be ri}}
                -english ... several people [can] play without having the game and {{the (can-be) computer for it}}.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la srasu
                -note This sentence is clipped from a much longer sentence. This sentence is in JACU. To convert it to CLL Lojban, substitute {{je}} with {{.e}}. 
                -noshownote The {{ka'e}} confuses me a little... I guess it's for specialized hardware for games that "can become a computer" by hacking? 
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi terve'u lo cnino {{skami be lo nu samtcifi'i}}
                -english I bought a new {{computer for programming}}.
                -source-id jbob
                -speaker la .zi.
                -note {{samtcifi'i}} expands to {{skami tutci finti}}, Creating computer-tools = programming.

-entry:
    -word smuni
    -gloss meaning, interpretation &mdash; (of an expression, of an event...) to mean, to be taken as ... &mdash; to interpret 
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 is a meaning/interpretation of x_2 recognized/seen/accepted by x_3.
            -note x<sub>1</sub> is normally an abstraction (most commonly a {{du'u}}); x<sub>2</sub> takes a wide range of types, with texts, desctiption, abstractions all being attested.
            -example:
                -lojban .i di'u zo'u ma {{smuni}} zo ta
                -english What's {{the meaning of}} {ta} in the last sentence?
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la nalvai
            -example:
                -lojban .i ru'a lo'u do cinmo ma doi ro do le'u {{se djica jai smuni}}
                -english I assume "do cinmo ma doi ro do" {{is what [you] want it to mean}}.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la janbe
                -note The {{jai}} raises the quote, {{lo'u do cinmo ma doi ro do le'u}}, into an abstraction so it can go into the x<sub>1</sub> place.
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo du'u da de natfe cu {{se smuni}} lo du'u da .o nai de ka'e jetnu
                -english "A contradicts B" {{means}} only one of A and B can be true.
                -source-id alic
                -note This sentence is localized from the original text, so it doesn't match literally to the original. The {{da}} and {{de}} are being used as generic pronouns, which is a common usage in texts from the 2000s.
            -example:
                -lojban .i la'e di'u cu {{se smuni}} lo barda mukti la .gregor.
                -english It {{is taken as}} a great motivation by Gregor.
                -source-id meta
            -example:
                -lojban .i krinu lo nu mabla ja dukse {{te smuni}} noi je'u fasnu ma'i lo ma'a cecmu
                -english This can lead to mis{{interpretation}} and/or over{{interpretation}} — and that is what happens in our community.
                -source-id offi
