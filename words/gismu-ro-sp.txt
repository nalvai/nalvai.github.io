
-entry:
    -word sance
    -gloss sound &mdash; to make sound, to produce sound
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 is sound produced/emitted by x_2.
            -example:
                -lojban .i .io la cevni zo'u doi lo zvati be vi ti ko gasnu lo nu {{sance fa lo gleki si'azgi}} 
                -english To God on high, all you in this place, let {{hymns ring out in joyful chorus}}!
                -source-id kogl
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi ba prami {{lo sance be lo brife be bu'u lo maxri}} 
                -english I shall love {{the sound of the wind in the wheat}}.
                -source-id litt
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo tubnu poi pu si'au trixe lo stizu cu ca cpana lo tuple be dy. je cu {{se sance lo sibli}}
                -english The tube that was seemingly behind the chair is now on his leg and {{hissing}}.
                -source-id nufa
                -note This examlpe is in JACU. To convert it to CLL/BPFK Lojban, substitute {{je cu}} with {{gi'e}}.

-entry:
    -word simlu
    -gloss to seem, to appear (to have a property) &mdash; semblance, (of a property) to seem to be present in ... &mdash; to perceive semblance &mdash; condition of semblance
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 seems/appears to have property(ies) x_2 to observer x_3 under conditions x_4.
            -note {{x_1 simlu x_2}} doesn't imply whether x_1 <i>actually</i> satisfy x_2 or not.
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo tsani cu {{simlu}} lo ka blanu
                -english The sky {{appears}} azure.
                -source-id care
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo zbasu noi manku cusku fo lo drata se zbasu cu {{simlu}} lo ka bacru lo jetnu voksa se pi'o lo junla
                -english The creator, who spoke obscurely in his other works, {{seemed}} to utter an authentic voice with the clock;
                -source-id cloc
            -example:
                -lojban .i je la .dorotis. cu {{te simlu}} fi vy fe lo ka desku milxe
                -english ..., and Dorothy {{thought (= percieved)}} it trembled a little.
                -source-id wond
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo noltruni'u ca lo nu lo plise cu bredi cu skari cnegau lo flira gi'e binxo {{lo simlu be lo ka te cange ninmu kei}} gi'e tai klama fo lo ze cmana fe lo ze torcrida
                -english When the apple was ready, the queen dyed her face and made herself {{like an Peasant Woman}} and so crossed the seven hills to the Dwarfs’ home.
                -source-id snow

-entry:
    -word smuni
    -gloss meaning, interpretation &mdash; (of an expression, of an event...) to mean, to be taken as ... &mdash; to interpret 
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 is a meaning/interpretation of x_2 recognized/seen/accepted by x_3.
            -note x<sub>1</sub> is normally an abstraction (most commonly a {{du'u}}); x<sub>2</sub> takes a wide range of types, with texts, desctiption, abstractions all being attested.
            -example:
                -lojban .i di'u zo'u ma {{smuni}} zo ta
                -english What's {{the meaning of}} {ta} in the last sentence?
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la nalvai
            -example:
                -lojban .i ru'a lo'u do cinmo ma doi ro do le'u {{se djica jai smuni}}
                -english I assume "do cinmo ma doi ro do" {{is what [you] want it to mean}}.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la janbe
                -note The {{jai}} raises the quote, {{lo'u do cinmo ma doi ro do le'u}}, into an abstraction so it can go into the x<sub>1</sub> place.
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo du'u da de natfe cu {{se smuni}} lo du'u da .o nai de ka'e jetnu
                -english "A contradicts B" {{means}} only one of A and B can be true.
                -source-id alic
                -note This sentence is localized from the original text, so it doesn't match literally to the original. The {{da}} and {{de}} are being used as generic pronouns, which is a common usage in texts from the 2000s.
            -example:
                -lojban .i la'e di'u cu {{se smuni}} lo barda mukti la .gregor.
                -english It {{is taken as}} a great motivation by Gregor.
                -source-id meta
            -example:
                -lojban .i krinu lo nu mabla ja dukse {{te smuni}} noi je'u fasnu ma'i lo ma'a cecmu
                -english This can lead to mis{{interpretation}} and/or over{{interpretation}} — and that is what happens in our community.
                -source-id offi
