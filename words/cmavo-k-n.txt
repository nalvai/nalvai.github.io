
-entry:
    -word ka'e
    -gloss Preposition (tense): innate capability
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho CAhA
        -meaning:
            -exp Innate capability: signifies an innate or potential capability that may or may not be present at the moment due to certain conditions.
            -note Innate capability is unrelated to the condition present at the moment. From BPFK Sections (without the Lojban parts): "[B]irds (except for few species) can fly but among them there are some that cannot do so now due to being crippled, too young, confined in small cage, etc."
            -example:
                -lojban .i va'i ro da poi {{ka'e se cusku}}
                -english That is, all that {{is able to be expressed}}.
                -source-id libr
            -example:
                -lojban .i co'a lo me ru crisa mi do jorne {{lo na ka'e se katna}}
                -english Starting that summer you and me were connected by {{something that can't be cut apart}}.
                -note The {{co'a}} follows the BPFK interpretation.
                -source-id pram
        -meaning:
            -variation Community
            -exp Actual capability: signifies capability present at the moment.
            -note Under this meaning, {{x<sub>1</sub> ka'e SELBRI x<sub>2</sub> x<sub>3</sub> ...}} is usually synonymous with {{x<sub>1</sub> kakne lo ka (ce'u) SELBRI x<sub>2</sub> x<sub>3</sub> ...}}.
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o le barda prenu ku zukte fi lo nu le cmalu ractu ku {{na ka'e cliva}}
                -english The big person made it so that the small rabbit {{was unable to leave}}.
                -source-id melb
        -meaning:
            -variation Compatible
            -exp Possibility: signifies the bridi is possible to be true.
            -example:
                -lojban {{ka'e ku}} lo prurai dinsauru cu cti'omni bu'u lo berti bolxadba
                -english First dinosaurs {{may}} have been omnivores in the north hemisphere
                -source-id firs
        -meaning:
            -variation Compatible
            -context When followed by a sumti
            -exp is possible under condition ...
            -note Defined in the Lojban definition of {{ka'e}} on jbovlaste, but isn't attested.


-entry:
    -word ke'u
    -gloss Attitudinal (discursive): again, repeating
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho UI3
        -meaning:
            -exp Express that the content is the same as that of something previously expressed.
            -example:
                -lojban ca'o ku zo'i sai lo cmana co kasakasas tricypezli cu cadzu fa re citno nanmu [...] .i {{ke'u}} zo'i sai nenri lo cmana
                -english Two young gentlemen were trekking through fallen leaves deep in the mountains. [...] They {{really}} were very deep in the mountains.
                -source-id rest
                -note This example uses CBM, where {{kasakasas}} functions as a brivla. The latter sentence (the one with {{ke'u}}) repeats the former sentence to reinforce the idea that "they were very deep in the mountains".
        -meaning:
            -context ~ {{nai}}
            -exp Express that something is different from something expressed earlier. 
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{ke'u nai}} loi djacu cu na rotsu i na ja ri na'e vlipa lo nu sarji lo barda bloti
                -english If water doesn't have sufficient depth, it will not have strength to support a large boat.
                -source-id care
                -note The text uses many different metaphors to allude to the same idea, with each metaphor being several sentences long. This example sentence is the start of a new metaphor, so it expresses something different from the preceding sentences.

-entry:
    -word la'o
    -gloss Quotation: start non-Lojban quotation as name
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho ZOI
        -meaning:
            -exp Begin non-Lojban name. The next Lojban word loses all its grammatical function, becomes the quote delimiter, and quotes all further text until the same word is repeated. The quote delimiters and the quoted text must be separated with pauses. The entire construct (from {{la'o}} to the second delimiter) is a sumti meaning "that named &lt;quoted text&gt;", and is considered a single word for grammatical purposes.
            -note The delimiter is usually chosen to give hints to the nature of the quoted text. For example, the first letter of the source language. {{zoi}} is often used as the default delimiter if no hints is to be given (in which case the construct is {{la'o zoi. &lt;quoted text&gt; .zoi}}).
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o ti datni papri fi'e {{la'o gy. blinry .gy.}} fi'o plijaspu {{la'o gy. CC0 .gy.}}
                -english Cheat sheet by {{blinry}}, (with license) {{CC0}}.
                -source-id tokp
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o salci {{la'o zoi León Werth zoi}} pe ca lo nu ly. verba 
                -english To {{León Werth}} when he was a little boy
                -source-id litt

-entry:
    -word li
    -gloss Mekso: forms number, convert methematical expression to sumti
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho LI
        -meaning:
            -exp Number. It converts a mathematical or numerical expression (MEX) into a sumti. The sumti refers to the value of the numerical expression. It is usually a numerical value, but it may be any referent of a numerical expression, for example a date. Terminate with {{lo'o}}.
            -example:
                -lojban .i ca ku .abu {{centre li so'u re mu}}
                -english ..., she was now {{only ten inches}} [high], ...
                -source-id alic
                -note The Lojban example translates literally to "... only 25 centimeters", as ten inches is about 25 centimeters. Combining definite and indefinite numbers into a single number implies that the two kinds of numbers have the same value in the given context (CLL 18.8), as seen in the {{so'u re mu}} in this example, where the {{re mu}} is taken to have the same value of {{so'u}} (few), thus the translation of "only 25". 
            -example:
                -lojban .i .e'o pensi ~ mi'e la uakci to'i de'i {{li re no pa ze ny pa ly mu dy}}
                -english Please [take some time to] consider this! ~ la uakci, {{January 5, 2017}}
                -source-id offi
                -note The mathematical expression is taken as a date. The {{ny}}, {{ly}}, {{dy}} stand for year, month, day respectively.
            -example:
                -lojban .i ku'i simlu lo ka sisku fi tu'a le daski kei ja'e le nu ti'u {{li pa ci pi'e re no vu'u pi'e pi'e dau}} cu klama le jai purci te zu'e tu'a le nalvai gluta pe le nei
                -english Wait a second, looks like he's looking for something in his pockets and at {{thirteen twenty minus ten seconds}} he goes back in for his crummy gloves.
                -source-id terr
                -note The mathematical expression is taken as a time.

    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho MOI
        -meaning:
            -exp Forms a selbri along with the preceding PA-string, lerfu-string or sumti. The resultant selbri means "x_1 is [number] in number/quantity".
            -note The rest of the place structure is difficult to determine, as different sources give conflicting definitions. Additionally, changes in the interpretation of articles (selma'o {{LE}}) made different structures surface as the same (because {{lo}} can be whatever), further complicating the determination of its uses. The rest of place structure is given as:<ul><li>jbovlaste, CLL (for subjective/inexact numbers): ... formed from a set x_2 of [number] members, one or more of which is/are x_3, measured relative to the set x_4.</li><li>CLL (for exact numbers): ... formed from a set x_2 of [number] members, one or more of which is/are x_3.</li><li>BPFK: ... and is among x_2.</li></ul>
            -example:
                -lojban .i vi {{pa mei}} ke blaci patxu
                -english ... with {{a [single]}} glass jar near it, ...
                -source-id rest 
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo jicmu na se jicmu vo'a to va'i lo pa klesi {{na ro mei}} toi
                -english The base is never the whole (the one dialect {{is not all [of the dialects]}}).
                -source-id offi
            -example:
                -lojban .i .ei kurji {{lo so'i mei be lo cipra}}
                -english I've {{experiments}} to run, ...
                -source-id stil
            -example:
                -lojban .i je se ki'u bo se cmene fa mi zo irmis voi me {{le te ci mei be le'i prije}}
                -english and for this I was called Hermes, {{thrice greatest in wisdom}}.
