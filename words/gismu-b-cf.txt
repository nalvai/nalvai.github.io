
-entry:
    -word bacru
    -gloss to speak, to utter &mdash; (words) spoken
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 utters verbally/says/phonates/speaks [vocally makes sound] x_2.
            -note Does not necessarily imply communication or audience. cf. {{cusku}}, which implies audience.
            -example:
                -lojban .i le ractu ku {{bacru}} zo coi
                -english The rabbit {{said}}: “Hey.”
                -source-id melb
            -example:
                -lojban .i ku'i ko'a na {{bacru}} lo cradybo'a .i lo minji cu {{bacru}}
                -english But the signals don't {{come from (= spoken by)}} them. [The signals] {{come from}} machines.
                -source-id made
                -note Note that the English sentences and the Lojban sentences have their arguments in opposite order. {{x_1 bacru x_2}} translates to "x_2 (signals) come from x_1".
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo zbasu noi manku cusku fo lo drata se zbasu cu simlu lo ka {{bacru lo jetnu voksa}} se pi'o lo junla
                -english The creator, who spoke obscurely in his other works, seemed in the clock to {{utter an authentic voice}}.
                -source-id cloc
            -example:
                -lojban .i mutce smaji .i sy tirna {{lo vasxu se bacru be lo gerku be'o}} .e nai lo drata
                -english It's very quiet. She heard {{dogs' whisperings}} and nothing else.
                -source-id nict

-entry:
    -word binxo
    -gloss to become, to change into ... &mdash; result of change &mdash; condition of change
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 becomes/changes/converts/transforms into x_2 under conditions x_3. 
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o lo jalge be lo nu ko'a lanli cu zi {{binxo}} lo te lijda be lo jdasi'a
                -english The queries of the philosopher soon {{became}} doctrines of the church.
                -source-id cloc
            -example:
                -lojban .i va'o lo nu gy djica lo nu polje pa lo tuple zo'u ri pa moi {{lo binxo be lo sirji}}
                -english If he wanted to bend one of his legs, then that was the first one {{to strech itself out}}.
                -note "strech itself out" = "become (something) straight"
                -source-id meta
            -example:
                -lojban .i le karce zo'a flecu klama fo le dargu pe le bartu noi {{ca'o manku binxo}}
                -english Cars slushed by on the street outside, where it {{was getting dark}}.
                -source-id ings
                -note An example of a very common tanru formation, where the seltau (modifying part of the tanru) describes the x_2 of the tertau (modified part of the tanru), which is "result of change" here. In this usage, {{manku binxo}} means similar to {{binxo lo manku}}.
            -example:
                -lojban .i xy. co'i {{makfa ke marji binxo}}
                -english They have {{become transmuted}} [instead].
                -source-id layo
                -note A counter-example of the above tanru formation pattern. Here, {{marji binxo}} doesn't mean {{binxo lo marji}} "become materialized", but "change in material" instead.
