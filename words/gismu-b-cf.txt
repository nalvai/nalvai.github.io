
-entry:
    -word bangu
    -gloss language &mdash; to use (a language) &mdash; to be expressed with a language
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 is a/the language/dialect used by x_2 to express/communicate x_3 (si'o/du'u, not quote).
            -note For "(of a text) to be written in a language", both x_2 and x_3 are attested, though using x_3 is more common. See quotes.
            -example:
                -lojban .i pe'i cizra milxe fa lo nu krici lo du'u lo jugbau cu {{bangu}} le ceizda 
                -english I think it's a little strange to believe that Chinese {{is the language of}} Heaven.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la nalvai
            -example:
                -lojban .i ku'i mu'a la'a cu'i so'o da {{co'a se bangu}} 
                -english But, for example, there might be some {{emerging speakers [of the language]}}.
                -source-id asse
            -example:
                -lojban .i le dei pagbu cu ro roi {{se bangu la .gliban}}.
                -english This page is always {{in English}}.
                -note Example of "(of a text) to be written in a language" expressed with x_2 of {{bangu}}.
                -source-id nict
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o da ze'u krici lo du'u lo tai selse'analka'e cukta cu {{te bangu}} fi lo prutce ja darno
                -english For a long time it was believed that those impenetrable books {{were in}} ancient or distant {{languages}}.
                -note Example of "(of a text) to be written in a language" expressed with x_3 of {{bangu}}.
                -source-id libr
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi jinvi lo du'u lo me zoi gy jbo.wikipedia.org gy cu bilga co {{te bangu fi lo jbobau}}
                -english I think stuffs in jbo.wikipedia.org should {{be in Lojban}}.
                -note Example of "(of a text) to be written in a language" expressed with x_3 of {{bangu}}.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la srasu

-entry:
    -word bebna
    -gloss silly, fool &mdash; folly, a foolish act
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 is foolish/silly in event/action/property [folly] (ka) x_2; x_1 is a fool.
            -example:
                -lojban .i ko co'a xenru {{doi bebna}}
                -english Start feeling some remorse, {{idiot.}}
                -source-id nafe
            -example:
                -lojban .i ku'i do mi dunli {{lo ka bebna}} 
                -english But you have been just as {{foolish}} as I.
                -source-id litt
            -example:
                -lojban .i .o'u no simsa {{se bebna}} pu fasnu
                -english No similar {{follies}} happened. Phew!
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la lunbe
            -example:
                -lojban .i le du'u xu kau {{bebna lo ka nerkla}} cu ba se facki ca le nu bevri fi le bartu
                -english Was {{[she] that dumb, did [she] go in}} or not, we'll see when they carry them out.
                -source-id terr

-entry:
    -word cadga
    -gloss (of an event) should happen &mdash; condition in which x<sub>1</sub> should happen &mdash; to think x<sub>1</sub> should happen
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 should/ought to be true if x_2 is true, according to x_3.
            -note Definition taken from <a href="https://wiki.lojban.io/zgadi">here</a>. Synonymous with {{zgadi}} (sense 1).
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{cadga}} fa lo ka kunti .ijeku'i co'a zgana mu'i lo ka viska lo kandi gusni
                -english Though it {{should have}} been empty, I started looking at it because I saw a faint light.
                -source-id denp
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{cadga}} fa lo nu do cusku lu la .lojban. li'u .a lu lo jbobau li'u
                -english You {{should}} say "la .lojban." or "lo jbobau".
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la lunra
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o la .varik. cu {{te cadga fi}} lo nu la'oi .Mastodon. joi zo'e cu zenba pe'a le ka ce'u selpli lo jbopre
                -english Varik {{thinks}} that Mastodon and some others {{should}} be used more by Lojbanists.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la varik

-entry:
    -word certu
    -gloss expert, competent in ..., skilled at ... &mdash; expertise, profession, skill &mdash; standard of skill
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 is an expert/pro/has prowess in/is skilled at x_2 (event/activity) by standard x_3.
            -example:
                -lojban .i li'a mi na {{certu}} lo ka sanga
                -english Obviously I'm not {{good at}} singing.
                -source-id pram
            -example:
                -lojban .i my se cau nai lo nu jerna cu ba'o binxo lo simlu {{be lo ka certu be ro cuntu poi srana la .gregor. bei lo rirni}}
                -english She had become used to the idea, not without reason, {{that she is an expert to his parents about the things that concerned Gregor}}. 
                -source-id meta
                -note The only usage I can find for {{certu}}'s x<sub>3</sub>.
            -example:
                -lojban .oi sai la'a lo nu frili ke cikna binxo cu vajni ke renvi {{se certu}} lo blupinxe
                -english Oww! Waking up easily is probably an important survival {{skill}} of a vampire. 
                -source-id nict
