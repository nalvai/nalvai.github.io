
-entry:
    -word bangu
    -gloss language &mdash; to use (a language) &mdash; to be expressed with a language
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 is a/the language/dialect used by x_2 to express/communicate x_3 (si'o/du'u, not quote).
            -note For "(of a text) to be written in a language", both x_2 and x_3 are attested, though using x_3 is more common. See quotes.
            -example:
                -lojban .i pe'i cizra milxe fa lo nu krici lo du'u lo jugbau cu {{bangu}} le ceizda 
                -english I think it's a little strange to believe that Chinese {{is the language of}} Heaven.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la nalvai
            -example:
                -lojban .i ku'i mu'a la'a cu'i so'o da {{co'a se bangu}} 
                -english But, for example, there might be some {{emerging speakers [of the language]}}.
                -source-id asse
            -example:
                -lojban .i le dei pagbu cu ro roi {{se bangu la .gliban}}.
                -english This page is always {{in English}}.
                -note Example of "(of a text) to be written in a language" expressed with x_2 of {{bangu}}.
                -source-id nict
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o da ze'u krici lo du'u lo tai selse'analka'e cukta cu {{te bangu}} fi lo prutce ja darno
                -english For a long time it was believed that those impenetrable books {{were in}} ancient or distant {{languages}}.
                -note Example of "(of a text) to be written in a language" expressed with x_3 of {{bangu}}.
                -source-id libr
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi jinvi lo du'u lo me zoi gy jbo.wikipedia.org gy cu bilga co {{te bangu fi lo jbobau}}
                -english I think stuffs in jbo.wikipedia.org should {{be in Lojban}}.
                -note Example of "(of a text) to be written in a language" expressed with x_3 of {{bangu}}.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la srasu

-entry:
    -word bebna
    -gloss silly, fool &mdash; folly, a foolish act
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 is foolish/silly in event/action/property [folly] (ka) x_2; x_1 is a fool.
            -example:

                -english Start feeling some remorse, {{idiot.}}
                -source-id nafe

                -source-id litt

                -lojban .i .o'u no simsa {{se bebna}} pu fasnu

                -source-id rolj

                -source-id terr


-entry:
    -word binxo
    -gloss to become, to change into ... &mdash; result of change &mdash; condition of change
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 becomes/changes/converts/transforms into x_2 under conditions x_3. 
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o lo jalge be lo nu ko'a lanli cu zi {{binxo}} lo te lijda be lo jdasi'a
                -english The queries of the philosopher soon {{became}} doctrines of the church.
                -source-id cloc
            -example:
                -lojban .i va'o lo nu gy djica lo nu polje pa lo tuple zo'u ri pa moi {{lo binxo be lo sirji}}
                -english If he wanted to bend one of his legs, then that was the first one {{to strech itself out}}.
                -note "strech itself out" = "become (something) straight"
                -source-id meta
            -example:
                -lojban .i le karce zo'a flecu klama fo le dargu pe le bartu noi {{ca'o manku binxo}}
                -english Cars slushed by on the street outside, where it {{was getting dark}}.
                -source-id ings
                -note An example of a very common tanru formation, where the seltau (modifying part of the tanru) describes the x_2 of the tertau (modified part of the tanru), which is "result of change" here. In this usage, {{manku binxo}} means similar to {{binxo lo manku}}.
            -example:
                -lojban .i xy. co'i {{makfa ke marji binxo}}
                -english They have {{become transmuted}} [instead].
                -source-id layo
                -note A counter-example of the above tanru formation pattern. Here, {{marji binxo}} doesn't mean {{binxo lo marji}} "become materialized", but "change in material" instead.




-entry:
    -word cadga
    -gloss (of an event) should happen &mdash; condition in which x<sub>1</sub> should happen &mdash; to think x<sub>1</sub> should happen
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 should/ought to be true if x_2 is true, according to x_3.
            -note Definition taken from <a href="https://wiki.lojban.io/zgadi">here</a>. Synonymous with {{zgadi}} (sense 1).
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{cadga}} fa lo ka kunti .ijeku'i co'a zgana mu'i lo ka viska lo kandi gusni
                -english Though it {{should have}} been empty, I started looking at it because I saw a faint light.
                -source-id denp
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{cadga}} fa lo nu do cusku lu la .lojban. li'u .a lu lo jbobau li'u
                -english You {{should}} say "la .lojban." or "lo jbobau".
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la lunra
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o la .varik. cu {{te cadga fi}} lo nu la'oi .Mastodon. joi zo'e cu zenba pe'a le ka ce'u selpli lo jbopre
                -english Varik {{thinks}} that Mastodon and some others {{should}} be used more by Lojbanists.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la varik


-entry:
    -word catlu
    -gloss to look at, to watch, to gaze at &mdash; to be looked at, to be watched
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 looks at/examines/views/inspects/regards/watches/gazes at x_2.
            -example:
                -lojban .i mutce jundi {{catlu}} ro da noi {{pu se catlu}}
                -english They very carefully {{looked}} everywhere that {{was looked at before}}.
                -source-id melb
            -example:
                -lojban .i ku'i nau ku cyny. co'a terpa gi je simxu {{catlu}} lo vo'a flira noi ba'o mutce co se gacri lo kruji
                -english But now, the two became freightened, and {{looked at}} each other's cream-covered face.
                -source-id rest
                -note This example uses a variant connective system. To convert it into CLL/BPFK Lojban, replace {{gi je}} with {{gi'e}}.
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo penmi be mi jo'u {{lo se catlu be mi}} cu ju'o cliva ba'i lo drata poi ba penmi ja catlu
                -english The people I meet now, {{the things I'm seeing now}}, they'll surely leave and replaced by what's ahead of me.
                -source-id lett




-entry:
    -word catra
    -gloss to kill, to murder &mdash; to be killed &mdash; method of killing
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 (agent) kills/slaughters/murders x_2 by action/method x_3.
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o {{lo catra be le nanmu goi ko'a}} cu du mi
                -english {{The killer of the man (called "ko'a" from now on)}} is me.
                -source-id inag
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo marde cu {{catra}} lo ma'a ka pacna kei ca lo za'i lo ma'a balvi cu se lebna
                -english Morals {{killed}} our hopes while our futures continue to be taken from us
                -source-id reed
            -example:
                -lojban .i lu ca ku ai sei ny cusku mi platu lo prane {{te catra}} li'u
                -english "Now," she said, "I'll devise the perfect {{plan to put an end [to her]}}!"
                -source-id snow

-entry:
    -word certu
    -gloss expert, competent in ..., skilled at ... &mdash; expertise, profession, skill &mdash; standard of skill
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 is an expert/pro/has prowess in/is skilled at x_2 (event/activity) by standard x_3.
            -example:
                -lojban .i li'a mi na {{certu}} lo ka sanga
                -english Obviously I'm not {{good at}} singing.
                -source-id pram
            -example:
                -lojban .i my se cau nai lo nu jerna cu ba'o binxo lo simlu {{be lo ka certu be ro cuntu poi srana la .gregor. bei lo rirni}}
                -english She had become used to the idea, not without reason, {{that she is an expert to his parents about the things that concerned Gregor}}. 
                -source-id meta
                -note The only usage I can find for {{certu}}'s x<sub>3</sub>.
            -example:
                -lojban .oi sai la'a lo nu frili ke cikna binxo cu vajni ke renvi {{se certu}} lo blupinxe
                -english Oww! Waking up easily is probably an important survival {{skill}} of a vampire. 
                -source-id nict
