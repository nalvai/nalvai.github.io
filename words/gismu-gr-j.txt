

-entry:
    -word janli
    -gloss to collide with ..., to bump into ... &mdash; to be collided into
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 collides with/crashes/bumps/runs into x_2.
            -example:
                -lojban .i su'o roi na mo'u klama gi'e sa'u {{janli}} lo tumla
                -english Sometimes [we] didn't reach it, but just {{drop to (= collide with)}} the ground [instead].
                -source-id care
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o do mi {{na ru'e janli}} fau lo nu mi muvycau sanli
                -english You {{almost bumped into}} me as I was standing there motionless.
                -source-id pram
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo xamsi boxna cu {{preja je janli}} lo drudi be lo bloti
                -english Waves were {{splashing and crashing}} over the deck.
                -source-id lian

-entry:
    -word jarco
    -gloss to show, to display &mdash; (of a property) to be shown, to be displayed &mdash; audience, to see ... being displayed
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 (agent) shows/exhibits/displays/[reveals]/demonstrates x_2 (property) to audience x_3.
            -note Although the definition says x_2 should be a property, concretes (like objects) in the x_2 place are atteseted (see examples below). See also {{.arco}}, which means the same as {{jarco}} but has its x_2 defined as a concrete.
            -example:
                -lojban .i ki'u ma lo junla zbasu na {{jarco}} vo'a
                -english Why does not the Clockmaker {{declare}} himself?
                -source-id cloc
            -example: 
                -lojban .i lo junla jo'u lo solri {{na jarco lo ka satci mapti simxu}}
                -english The clock and the sun were {{in no precise accordance}}.
                -source-id cloc
            -example: 
                -lojban .i pau xamgu fa lo nu ma te {{jarco le mi rarna}}
                -english So who should [I] {{show my true self}} to?
                -source-id guit

    -word jgari
    -gloss to hold, to grasp &mdash; to be held &mdash; (of a part of x<sub>1</sub>) to be used for holding ... &mdash; (of a part of x<sub>2</sub>) to be held at
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 grasps/holds/clutches/seizes/grips/[hugs] x_2 with x_3 (part of x_1) at locus x_4 (part of x_2).
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{bevri jgari}} lo solri lo jgalu
                -english [He] {{had}} the sun in one claw;
                -source-id cloc
            -example:
                -lojban .i le smani poi se kerlo pa da zi'e noi xendo cu {{jgari}} le cifnu lo birka
                -english The ape with the one ear, who was a good natured fellow, {{held}} the baby in his arms;

            -example:
                -lojban .i se ni'i bo ra pu'o canci sau lo nu {{tsali jgari fo le mi xance}}
                -english So before it disappears, [you] need to {{grip my hand tight}}.
                -source-id teoq

-entry:
    -word jimpe
    -gloss to understand, to comprehend (a fact) &mdash; fact understood &mdash; subject of understanding
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 understands/comprehends fact/truth x_2 (du'u) about subject x_3; x1 understands (fi) x3.
            -note Place structure parallels that of {{krici}}. Lacks a sumti slot for epistemology/grounds (corresponing to x<sub>4</sub> of {{djuno}}) compared to its near-synonym {{djuno}}. 
            -example:
                -lojban mi {{jimpe}} lo du'u do ma kau cinmo
                -english I {{understand}} how you feel.
                -source-id wond
            -example:
                -lojban .i .a bu snada lo ka {{jimpe}} tu'a lo valsi
                -english She (Alice) made out the words.
                -source-id alic
                -note Make out the words = Succeed to understand the words.
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{jimpe}} fi lo selcni .i .au kelci ne'i le xamsi pe'a
                -english {{Understanding}} emotions, I want to play in the (figurative) ocean.
                -source-id diam
            -example:
                -lojban .i do terpa lo nu mi jai gau xrani do se pi'o {{lo se jimpe be mi}}
                -english You fear that I'll cause harm to you with {{my understandings}}.
                -source-id nict

-entry:
    -word jinvi
    -gloss to opine, to hold (an opinion) &mdash; opinion &mdash; subject of opinion &mdash; basis/grounds of opinion 
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 thinks/opines x_2 [opinion] (du'u) is true about subject/issue x_3 on grounds x_4.
            -note Place structure parallels that of {{djuno}}. Has an additional sumti slot for epistemology/grounds (x<sub>4</sub>) compared to its near-synonym {{krici}}. 
            -example:
                -lojban lo patfu cu {{jinvi}} fi gy. fe lo du'u mapti fa lo po'o traji jursa
                -english [...] his father {{thought}} it fitting to always be extremely strict with him. 
                -source-id meta
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o mi {{jinvi}} fo lo se junri fe lo du'u lo plini poi terdi lo cmalu noltru cu me lo plinycma po'u li B612
                -english I have serious reason {{to believe}} that the planet from which the little prince came is the asteroid known as B-612.
                -source-id litt
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o vajni lo nabmi fa lo za'i {{lo se jinvi be fi lo du'u ma kau jbobau ku ku}} frica simxu
                -english The relevant part of this problem is the difference of {{opinions on what is considered a Lojban}}.
                -source-id offi
                -note Using two {{ku}} consecutively, as in this sentence, is considered by most to be poor style because it can be difficult to determine which grammatical structure each {{ku}} closes. In this sentence, replacing the two {{ku}} with a {{cu}} keeps the same sentence structure and reduces the cognitive load of backtracking.

-entry:
    -word jitro
    -gloss to control, to harness, to direct &mdash; to be controlled &mdash; controlled action/activity/property 
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 has control over/harnesses/manages/directs/conducts x_2 in x_3 (activity/event/performance).
            -example:
                -lojban .i ba'a mi ze'e {{se jitro do}} po'o
                -english My [soul] will always {{be}} only {{yours}}
                -source-id amap
            -example:
                -lojban .i mu'i bo mi platu lo nu {{jitro}} cy. tai tu'a lo xirma gi'e gasnu lo nu cy. cu gunka
                -english ..., for I have a mind to {{harness}} him like a horse, and make him work.
                -source-id wond
            -example:
                -lojban cedra lo nu lo'e cnino joi lo'e citno cu nitcu lo ka {{ce'u kau jitro}} lo jbobau joi lo kulnu pe ri lo ka farvi je lo ka banro
                -english It's an era in which newcomers and young people need to {{be the directors of}} Lojban and its culture, shaping how it evolves and grows.
                -source-id jbob
                -speaker la solpa'i
                -note This sentence is in JACU. To convert it to CLL/BPFK Lojban, substitute {{je}} with {{.e}}.
