-entry:
    -word zanfu'a
    -gloss serendipity, (of an event) fortunate, lucky &mdash; (of a person) fortunate, lucky
    -type lujvo
    -origin zabna funca
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 happens by good luck of x_2; x_1 is a case of serendipity happening to x_2.
            -note For its swapped form, {{zanselfu'a}} is more common than {{se zanfu'a}} colloquially.
            -example:
                -lojban .i tu'a do {{zanfu'a mi}}
                -english {{I was so lucky}} with you.
                -source-id koid
            -example:
                -lojban .i sy. {{se zanfu'a}} lo nu ba zi zifre barkla lo sy. selri'u noi se tcita li 701
                -english He {{had the fortune}} to escape at an early period of his case, which was numbered 701.
                -source-id scie
            -example:
                -lojban .i le jai mau cmalu cu {{se zanfu'a}} lo ka cpana le marce
                -english The smaller one, he{{'s lucky}}, mounts his scooter, ...
                -source-id terr
