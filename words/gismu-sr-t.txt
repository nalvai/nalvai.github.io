    -word srera
    -gloss to err, to make mistake(s) &mdash; error, mistake &mdash; condition/environment of mistake &mdash; standard of mistake 
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 errs in doing/being/making mistake x_2 (event), an error under conditions x_3 by standard x_4.
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi'a pu {{srera}} lo nu sombo lo blabi
                -english We planted a white one {{by mistake}}.
                -source-id alic
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi ba {{srera}} tu'a lo vajni mutce tcila
                -english In certain more important details I shall {{make mistakes}}.
                -source-id litt
            -example:
                -lojban .i mlauca lo klesi be lo jbopre poi jinvi lo du'u lo jbobau cu ve tavla fi po'o lo gerna ja lo nu dragau {{lo se srera}}
                -english It mentions a type of Lojbanists who think Lojban is only for talking about grammar or correcting {{[other's] mistakes}}.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker Lucilla
            -example:
                -lojban .i .u'uru'e mi {{srera}} fi le nu dramau fa le nu cusku lu do tadni fi ma li'u
                -english Oops, I {{made a mistake}}, saying "do tadni fi ma" would be more correct.
                -source-id jbob
                -speaker la kanba
                -note The previous sentence is the same speaker saying {{.i do tadni ma}}. Interestingly, {{tadni}} doesn't have its x<sub>3</sub> defined, so it's unclear what their intended meaning is.
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o ky klasiko {{srera}} fo pa ky nupre be lo nu nonroi gasnu
                -english He'd made the classic {{mistake}}, the one he'd sworn he'd never make.
                -source-id neur

-entry:
    -word traji
    -gloss most, superlative &mdash; superlative property &mdash; direction of superlativity &mdash; range of superlativity
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 is superlative in property x_2 (ka), the x_3 extreme (ka; default {{lo ka zmadu}}) among set/range x_4.
            -note Place structure parallels that of {{mutce}}, with an additional x_4 place for the range x_1 is superlative in.
            -example:
                -lojban .i ki'u bo fi tu'a ra {{traji fo le'i gusni}}
                -english For with it {{is the light of lights}}.
                -source-id emer
            -example:
                -lojban .i jimpe fe lo du'u do cu {{traji lo ka xendo kei fo lo'i prenu .ia}}
                -english I understand that you {{are kinder than all those people}}.
                -source-id odds
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 is superlative in property x_2 (ka) among set/range x_3.
            -note Lacks the direction of extremeness compared to the above sense. Note that using non-gimste definition and using reform grammar usually come together, so the x_3 under this usage usually isn't a set per CLL Lojban (formed with {{lo'i}}/{{le'i}}), but a plural term (as used by most reformists, formed implicitly with {{lo}} under plural-logic Lojban). See the below two examples, where the x_3 of {{traji}} is filled with {{lo}}-description.
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o lo ziptu'a cu {{traji lo ka lenku ku lo ro stuzi pe lo munje}}
                -english Antarctica may {{be the world's frostiest place}}, ...
                -source-id anta
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{lo tinsa traji be fi lo zifre pensi}} cu sanji lo sivni nu senpi
                -english ..., and {{the most solid of the free thinkers}} was conscious of a private doubt.
                -source-id cloc

-entry:
    -word troci
    -gloss to try, to attempt &mdash; goal &mdash; effort, trial  
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 tries/attempts/makes an effort to do/attain x_2 (event/state/property) by actions/method x_3.
            -note Place structure parallels that of {{rivbi}}. The x<sub>3</sub> place is seldom used. {{x<sub>1</sub> troci x<sub>2</sub> x<sub>3</sub>}} is similar in meaning to {{x<sub>1</sub> zukte x<sub>3</sub> x<sub>2</sub>}}.
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi co'u {{troci lo nu fagysti}}
                -english I'm done {{trying to put out fires}}.
                -source-id nabm
            -example:
                -lojban .i ku'i lo prami {{na'o troci}} ba'e ju certu lo nandu
                -english But people who love {{try}} difficult things no matter whether they are good at them.
                -source-id pram
            -example:
                -lojban zo ie pe mi pu {{jai te troci lonu mi dragau tu'a mi}}
                -english That <b>ie</b> of mine was {{me trying to correct myself}}.
                -source-id jbob
                -speaker Stephen Dedalus
                -note The only usage I can find for {{troci}}'s x<sub>3</sub>.
