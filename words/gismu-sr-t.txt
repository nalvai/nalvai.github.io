


-entry:
    -word tavla
    -gloss to tell, to talk to &mdash; to be talked to &mdash; subject of talk &mdash; language used  
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 talks/speaks to x_2 about subject x_3 in language x_4.
            -example:
                -lojban .i la .cíluuax. .ainmo lo ka {{tavla}} la cmalu le nu cy. fliba
                -english Sheelua intended to {{talk}} to Tiny about her (= Tiny's) failures.
                -source-id clan
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o la .stefen. co'a sanga gi'e {{tavla}} fi lo nu litru vau la .gliban.
                -english Stefen started to sing and {{talked}} about the journey in English.
                -source-id nict
                -noshownote The original has {{je}} instead of {{gi'e}}



-entry:
    -word troci
    -gloss to try, to attempt &mdash; goal &mdash; effort, trial  
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 tries/attempts/makes an effort to do/attain x_2 (event/state/property) by actions/method x_3.
            -note The x<sub>3</sub> place is seldom used. {{x<sub>1</sub> troci x<sub>2</sub> x<sub>3</sub>}} is similar in meaning to {{x<sub>1</sub> zukte x<sub>3</sub> x<sub>2</sub>}}.
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi co'u {{troci lo nu fagysti}}
                -english I'm done {{trying to put out fires}}.
                -source-id nabm
            -example:
                -lojban .i ku'i lo prami {{na'o troci}} ba'e ju certu lo nandu
                -english But people who love {{try}} difficult things no matter whether they are good at them.
                -source-id pram
            -example:
                -lojban zo ie pe mi pu {{jai te troci lonu mi dragau tu'a mi}}
                -english That <b>ie</b> of mine was {{me trying to correct myself}}.
                -source-id jbob
                -speaker Stephen Dedalus
                -note The only usage I can find for {{troci}}'s x<sub>3</sub>.
