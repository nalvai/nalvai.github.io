    -word mapti
    -gloss suitable, to match, to fit &mdash; what x<sub>1</sub> matches, what x<sub>1</sub> is suitable for &mdash; aspect/property where x<sub>1</sub> fits
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 fits/matches/suits/is compatible/appropriate/corresponds to/with x_2 in property/aspect x_3.
            -example:
                -lojban .i lu mi na fengu li'u co'u {{mapti}} lo fatci
                -english The sentence "I'm not angry" no longer {{matches}} the actualities.
                -source-id nafe
            -example:
                -lojban za'a dai mi na'e ke clira {{mapti}} fi lo ka kelci 
                -english As you can see, I'm not {{suitable}} for playing [because it's] too late [here now].
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la uakci
                -note The main selbri, {{clira mapti}}, translated literally to "early-suitable", which is taken to be "be early enough to be suitable" in the translation given above.
