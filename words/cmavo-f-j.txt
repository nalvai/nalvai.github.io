    -word fai
    -gloss Place Structure: tag original x<sub>1</sub> swapped out by jai
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho FA
        -meaning:
            -exp Sumti place tag: tag a sumti moved out of numbered place structure by {{jai}}.
            -note The meaning of {{fai}} without {{jai}} is undefined. BPFK claims {{fai}} has a rafsi form {{-fax-}}, but the rafsi is taken by {{fraxu}}.
            -example:
                -lojban .i ba zi bo .abu {{jai suksa fai}} lo nu pejgau lo xance gi'e jgari lo vacri
                -english ..., and then she {{suddenly}} spread out her hand, and made a snatch in the air.
                -source-id alic
            -example:
                -lojban .i ku'i lo cabdei ku jai ca fasnu {{fai lo nu ba zi retsku fa mi}}
                -english But today is the day {{I going to ask}}.
                -source-id koid
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo jai ja'e dzicatlu {{be fai py.}} lo jai ja'e galcatlu {{be fai py.}} cu simsa pe'i
                -english The effect {{of the bird}} looking down would be just the same as to that (the effect) {{of [the bird]}} looking up.
                -source-id care

    -word fe'e
    -gloss Preposition (tense) modifier: mark spatial tense
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho FEhE
        -meaning:
            -exp Converts the following time tag into its space equivalent. The following time tag must be one of {{ZAhO}}, {{TAhE}} or {{number + ROI}}. 
            -example:
                -lojban .i dasni zei mulno lo crino {{fe'e co'a}} lo stedu {{fe'e co'u}} lo jamfu
                -english [He was] clothed all in green, {{from}} his head {{to}} his feet.
                -source-id wond
                -note The {{co'a}} and {{co'u}} follow the BPFK interpretation.
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi vofli {{fe'eru'inai}} ve'a lo mulno munje
                -english I have flown {{a little}} over all parts of the world.
                -source-id litt
                -contributor la lalxu
            -example:
                -lojban .i lu ge'e cizra zdani .i .ua nai ki'u ma {{fe'e so'i roi}} vorme li'u
                -english "What a weird building. Why does it {{have so many}} doors?"
                -source-id rest

-entry:
    -word fi'o
    -gloss Preposition: converts selbri into preposition
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho FIhO
        -meaning:
            -exp Converts the following selbri into a modal/tag/preposition. The resulting preposition introduces an unnumbered sumti that plays the role of the converted selbri's x<sub>1</sub> in the sentence.
            -note The exact relation between the introduced sumti and the rest of the sentence has not been rigorously defined and is usually understood in an ad-hoc manner. The same goes for BAI prepositions.
            -example:
                -lojban .i do prane ro zu'i {{fi'o morji le do panzi}}
                -english {{Your kids will remember}} you as perfect in every way.
                -source-id eggq
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o ti datni papri fi'e la'o gy. blinry .gy. {{fi'o plijaspu}} la'o gy. CC0 .gy.
                -english Cheat sheet by blinry, {{with license}} CC0.
                -source-id tokp
            -example:
                -lojban .i viska bu'u lo cmana lo mrovau .e la snime blabi ku noi melbi ku'o gi'e tcidu lo se ciska {{be fi'o lerfu lo solji}}
                -english He saw the coffin on the mountain and lovely Snow White inside, and read what was written {{in golden letters}}.
                -source-id snow
            -example:
                -lojban .iku'i la jbovlaste cu na {{jai fi'o ve vreji fe'u smuni}} fai da zo ninja
                -english But there aren't any meaning of "ninja" {{recorded in}} jbovlaste.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la srasu
                -note The sumti slot introduced by {{fi'o ve vreji fe'u}} is promoted to x<sub>1</sub> by {{jai}}.
                -message-link https://discord.com/channels/771781383361921024/771782618856947752/807012280343461888
                -timestamp 2021-02-05

-entry:
    -word ja'o
    -gloss Attitudinal (evidential): I conclude
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho UI2
        -meaning:
            -exp Evidential: used to express ideas or statements that were not sourced, but rather deduced by the speaker from other sources or statements.
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{ja'o}} lo danlu poi xabju lo djacu cu ca'o xabju lo bartu be lo djacu .i ia nai
                -english {{And it comes to this;}} a creature that lives in water living out of water!
                -source-id cloc
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{ja'o}} mi stidi ru'a lo du'u pilno fi lo ka te cmene kei fe lu jicmu lojbo li'u
                -english {{Thus,}} I propose that we consequently use the name «Base Lojban» or «Fundamental Lojban».
                -source-id offi
            -example:
                -lojban .i lu {{ja'o}} ma'a ba kavbu lo cifnu li'u
                -english "{{And therefore}} we shall take babies," [concluded the doctor.]
                -source-id scie

-entry:
    -word jai
    -gloss Place Structure: promote other sumti slot to x<sub>1</sub>
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho JAI
        -meaning:
            -context before a selbri/tanru-unit
            -exp Sumti raising: replaces the meaning of the selbri's original x_1 with "some proposition/event/preperty... involved in x_1". The original x_1 is moved to the {{fai}} place. {{ko'a jai broda}} is equivalent to {{tu'a ko'a (lu'u) broda}}. Used when the selbri takes an abstraction as x_1, but a concrete (object etc.) is desired for the place. 
            -note Note that {{jai}} acts on tanru unit (instead of selbri as a whole), so {{jai broda brode}} will have the exact same place strcutre as {{brode}}.
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi {{na jai se curmi fai}} lo nu fitytu'i da
                -english I {{am not allowed to}} let anyone in.
                -source-id snow
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o {{lo jai daspo}} cu tolcliva
                -english {{The destroyer}} arrives.
                -source-id nict
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo pulji cu {{jai lerci}} dukse
                -english The police are too {{late}}.
                -source-id maze
                -note {{lo pulji}} is <u>not</u> raised in this sentence because {{jai lerci}} is in the seltau (modifying) position of the tanru and thus have no effect on the place strucure of the selbri.
        -meaning:
            -context before a preposition
            -exp Replaces the selbri's original x_1 with the sumti introduced by the following preposition. The original x_1 is moved to the {{fai}} place.  
            -note Note that {{jai}} acts on tanru unit (instead of selbri as a whole), so {{jai PREPOSITION broda brode}} will have the exact same place strcutre as {{brode}}.
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{jai gau sepli fa ko}} fai le terdi fe le fagri
                -english {{Separate}} the earth from the fire. 
                -source-id emer
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{lo jai ca cliva}} cu jibni
                -english {{The hour of his departure}} drew near.
                -source-id litt
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{lo jai ja'e dzicatlu be fai py.}} {{lo jai ja'e galcatlu be fai py.}} cu simsa pe'i
                -english {{The effect to the bird looking down}} would be just the same as to {{that (the effect) of looking up}}.
                -source-id care

-entry:
    -word je'i
    -gloss Connective: selbri/tanru-unit afterthought connective question
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho JA
        -meaning:
            -exp Logical connective: tanru-internal afterthought connective question. Connects two tanru units (or other grammatical structures) and asks the listener to supply an afterthought connective that makes the containing bridi true.
            -note See {{je}} for a list of what grammatical structures a member of {{JA}} can connect. Supplanted with {{ji}} in JACU.
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo blanu cu se jetnu skari ra {{.i je'i}} ra darno gi'e kuspe ve'u fe'e za'o
                -english Is its azure the proper colour{{? Or}} is it occasioned by its distance and illimitable extent{{?}}
                -source-id care
            -example:
                -lojban &mdash; ma xe'ei jbonunjmaji ti'u lo cabna'a<br>&mdash; vonma'i {{je'i}} duvma'i jbonunjmaji
                -english &mdash; Where is this year's Lojban-gathering?<br>&mdash; April {{or}} October Lojban-gathering{{?}}
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker portal 2056, la nalvai
                -note {{xe'ei}} is an abstractor (selma'o {{NU}}) meaning "x_1 is the place where [bridi] takes place".
                -message-link https://discord.com/channels/771781383361921024/779079939462922270/1399343175235141747
                -timestamp 2025-07-28
            -example:
                -lojban .i xu lo fagri cu daspo lo do zdani .i .a'o na go'i .i ca'o {{je'i}} ba'o fagri
                -english Did the fire destroy your home? I hope it didn't. Is the fire still going, {{or}} has it already ended{{?}}
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la .ilmen.
                -timestamp 2024-11-14
                -message-link https://discord.com/channels/771781383361921024/1304486402418937931/1306314262498770965
