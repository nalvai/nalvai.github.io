
-entry:
    -word fe'e
    -gloss Preposition (tense) modifier: mark spatial tense
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho FEhE
        -meaning:
            -exp Converts the following time tag into its space equivalent. The following time tag must be one of {{ZAhO}}, {{TAhE}} or {{number + ROI}}. 
            -example:
                -lojban .i dasni zei mulno lo crino {{fe'e co'a}} lo stedu {{fe'e co'u}} lo jamfu
                -english [He was] clothed all in green, {{from}} his head {{to}} his feet.
                -source-id wond
                -note The {{co'a}} and {{co'u}} follow the BPFK interpretation.


-entry:
    -word fi'e
    -gloss Preposition: created by...
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho BAI
        -meaning:
            -exp Created by ...; Introduces an unnumbered sumti that plays the role of {{finti}}'s x<sub>1</sub> in the sentence.
            -note The event described by the bridi is normally associated with the creation of something, and that the creator is the referent of the tagged sumti. Often seen in titles to indicate the author of a text. 
            -example:
                -lojban ki'e {{jai fi'e}} mupli
                -english Thanks, example-{{creator}}!
                -source-id jbob
                -speaker la fagri
            -example:
                -lojban clani kerlo {{fi'e}} la fagri
                -english Long Ears, {{by}} Fagri
                -source-id clan
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi jinvi lo du'u so'i cnino zmadu zgike {{be fi'e mi}} cu zabna
                -english I think many newer songs {{by me}} are nice.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la janbe
            -example:
                -lojban .i gidva ro da fa lo se platu {{be fi'e mi}}
                -english {{My}} plans were guiding everything.
                -source-id calc
        -meaning:
            -context {{se}} ~
            -exp Creating work ...; Introduces an unnumbered sumti that plays the role of {{finti}}'s x<sub>2</sub> in the sentence.
            -note The event described by the bridi is associated with the creation of the referent of the tagged sumti. {{se fi'e}} is unattested.
        -meaning:
            -context {{te}} ~
            -exp Creating with purpose ...; Introduces an unnumbered sumti that plays the role of {{finti}}'s x<sub>3</sub> in the sentence.
            -note The event described by the bridi is associated with some event of creation, for a purpose indicated by the referent of the tagged sumti.
            -example:
                -lojban .i pacna {{te fi'e}} po'o lo nu kulgu'a snada
                -english I {{invented}} [dreams] merely {{in hopes of}} completing [my] homework. 
                -source-id rout
        -meaning:
            -context {{ve}} ~
            -exp Creating from ...; Introduces an unnumbered sumti that plays the role of {{finti}}'s x<sub>4</sub> in the sentence.
            -note The event described by the bridi is associated with some event of creation, using pre-existing ideas or elements indicated by the referent of the tagged sumti. 
            -example:
                -lojban lo du'u nandu fa lo nu cusku lu'e lo smuni poi simsa zo javni ku'o {{vefi'e}} lo drata smuni cu nibli pe'i lo du'u le javni smuni cu xamgu zmadu
                -english The difficulty in expressing something similar to "javni" {{from}} other meanings means that the official meaning is better IMO. 
                -source-id jbob
                -speaker _4Denthusiast


-entry:
    -word fi'o
    -gloss Preposition: converts selbri into preposition
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho FIhO
        -meaning:
            -exp Converts the following selbri into a modal/tag/preposition. The resulting preposition introduces an unnumbered sumti that plays the role of the converted selbri's x<sub>1</sub> in the sentence.
            -note The exact relation between the introduced sumti and the rest of the sentence has not been rigorously defined and is usually understood in an ad-hoc manner. The same goes for BAI prepositions.
            -example:
                -lojban .i do prane ro zu'i {{fi'o morji le do panzi}}
                -english {{Your kids will remember}} you as perfect in every way.
                -source-id eggq
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o ti datni papri fi'e la'o gy. blinry .gy. {{fi'o plijaspu}} la'o gy. CC0 .gy.
                -english Cheat sheet by blinry, {{with license}} CC0.
                -source-id tokp
            -example:
                -lojban .i viska bu'u lo cmana lo mrovau .e la snime blabi ku noi melbi ku'o gi'e tcidu lo se ciska {{be fi'o lerfu lo solji}}
                -english He saw the coffin on the mountain and lovely Snow White inside, and read what was written {{in golden letters}}.
                -source-id snow
            -example:
                -lojban .iku'i la jbovlaste cu na {{jai fi'o ve vreji fe'u smuni}} fai da zo ninja
                -english But there aren't any meaning of "ninja" {{recorded in}} jbovlaste.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la srasu
                -note The sumti slot introduced by {{fi'o ve vreji fe'u}} is promoted to x<sub>1</sub> by {{jai}}.


-entry:
    -word ge'i
    -gloss Connective: forethought connective question
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho GA
        -meaning:
            -exp Logical connective: all but tanru-internal forethought connective question. Asks the listener to supply a FLC that makes the containing statement true.
            -note Supplanted with {{ga ji}} in JACU.
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o xu lo prenu co vajni cu {{ge'i}} ca ranji lo nu kansa ku {{gi}} ve'u sepli do gi'e pu co'a cliva fi lo drata se litru
                -english As for the people important [to you], do they continue to accompany you{{? Or}} are they now distant and separated from you, having departed via their own paths{{?}}
                -source-id lett

-entry:
    -word jai
    -gloss Place Structure: promote other sumti slot to x<sub>1</sub>
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho JAI
        -meaning:
            -context before a selbri/tanru-unit
            -exp Sumti raising: replaces the meaning of the selbri's original x_1 with "some proposition/event/preperty... involved in x_1". The original x_1 is moved to the {{fai}} place. {{ko'a jai broda}} is equivalent to {{tu'a ko'a (lu'u) broda}}. Used when the selbri takes an abstraction as x_1, but a concrete (object etc.) is desired for the place. 
            -note Note that {{jai}} acts on tanru unit (instead of selbri as a whole), so {{jai broda brode}} will have the exact same place strcutre as {{brode}}.
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi {{na jai se curmi fai}} lo nu fitytu'i da
                -english I {{am not allowed to}} let anyone in.
                -source-id snow
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o {{lo jai daspo}} cu tolcliva
                -english {{The destroyer}} arrives.
                -source-id nict
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo pulji cu {{jai lerci}} dukse
                -english The police are too {{late}}.
                -source-id maze
                -note {{lo pulji}} is <u>not</u> raised in this sentence because {{jai lerci}} is in the seltau (modifying) position of the tanru and thus have no effect on the place strucure of the selbri.
        -meaning:
            -context before a preposition
            -exp Replaces the selbri's original x_1 with the sumti introduced by the following preposition. The original x_1 is moved to the {{fai}} place.  
            -note Note that {{jai}} acts on tanru unit (instead of selbri as a whole), so {{jai PREPOSITION broda brode}} will have the exact same place strcutre as {{brode}}.
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{jai gau sepli fa ko}} {{fai le terdi}} fe le fagri
                -english {{Separate}} {{the earth}} from the fire. 
                -source-id emer
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{lo jai ca cliva}} cu jibni
                -english {{The hour of his departure}} drew near.
                -source-id litt
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{lo jai ja'e dzicatlu be fai py.}} {{lo jai ja'e galcatlu be fai py.}} cu simsa pe'i
                -english {{The effect to the bird looking down}} would be just the same as to {{that (the effect) of looking up}}.
                -source-id care

-entry:
    -word ji
    -gloss Connective: sumti afterthought connective question
    -type cmavo
    -rafsi-cvc
    -rafsi-ccv 
    -rafsi-cvv 
    -definition:
        -selmaho A
        -meaning:
            -exp Logical connective: sumti afterthought connective question. Connects two sumti and asks the listener to supply a sumti afterthought connective that makes the containing bridi true. 
            -example:
                -lojban cacra li re vo pe'i {{ji}} li pa re pei
                -english Twenty-four hours, I think; {{or}} is it twelve{{?}}
                -source-id alic
    -definition:
        -selmaho JA
        -meaning:
            -variation JACU
            -exp Connective: connective question. Connects two grammatical constructs of the same type and asks the listener to supply a connective that makes the containing bridi true. 
            -example:
                -lojban ― coi jbope'o mi'e zi sipna <br> ― puzi {{ji}} bazi <br> ― naje li'abu'o u'i
                -english ― Hello Lojban friends, I'm sleeping (from a short distance in time from now) <br> ― To the past {{or}} to the future? <br> ― The latter, should be obvious now, haha.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la srasu, la evis
                -note The middle sentence would be {{puzi <u>je'i</u> bazi}} under CLL Lojban.




-entry:
    -word ji'a
    -gloss Attitudinal (discursive): additionally
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho UI3b
        -meaning:
            -exp Discursive: indicate an additional case or additional cases.
            -note Keywords include "also", "too", "additionally", "in addition", "as well", "moreover", "furthermore", "to boot", "even".
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo munje cu palci je'u .i {{ji'a}} lo nu jmive cu nibli lo nu cortu
                -english The world is truly evil, {{and}} living implies suffering.
                -source-id kuhi
