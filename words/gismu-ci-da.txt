-entry:
    -word cmavo
    -gloss structure word &mdash; grammatical class &mdash; meaning/function of the word &mdash; usage/language of the word
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 is a structure word of grammatical class x_2, with meaning/function x_3 in usage (language) x_4.
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o {{zoi ty. e .ty cmavo fi lo du'u lo lifri}} cu sepli le drata pagbu be le jufra
                -english {{The particle <i>e</i>, which [introduces] a direct object}}, separates it from the rest of the sentence.
                -source-id tokp
                -note The grammatical term "direct object" is translated as {{lo lifri}}, lit. "experiencer".
            -example:
                -lojban .i xu la .lucil. cu jinvi le du'u lo nu jimpe cu se filri'a lo nu pilno {{lo cmavo be le cmavrfa}} tu'a lo no sumti jonai lo ro sumti
                -english Does Lucilla thinks understanding is made easy when either none or all of the sumti is [tagged] with {{FA (cmavo of class FA)}}?
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la varik
                -note This sentence is in JACU. To convert it to CLL Lojban, substitute {{jonai}} with {{.onai}}. Also, {{cmavrfa}} is a type-3 zi'evla. {{cmavrfa}} = cmav-r-FA.
            -example:
                -lojban .icaku mi djica tu'a {{lo cmavo be ma'oi ca'a bei tu'a zo je'u}}
                -english Now I want {{a CAhA (cmavo of class CAhA) that means similarly to <i>je'u</i>}}.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la srasu
            -example:
                -lojban .i so'ida .oiru'e {{temci cmavo fo lo jbobau}}
                -english There are so many {{tense particles in Lojban...}}
                -source-id jbob
                -speaker la melmi
