-entry:
    -word ba
    -gloss Preposition (tense): after ...; future tense
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho PU
        -meaning:
            -exp Future: signifies the event happens / the proposition holds at some point after the reference point. 
            -note When used alone in main clauses, the reference point is usually that of the speaker, which makes {{ba}} function like a future tense marker. In subordinate clauses, the reference point is the point in spacetime at which the outer clause takes place. Note that {{ba}} alone says nothing about whether the sentence is true now or in the past.
            -example:
                -lojban mi {{ba}} citka ma
                -english What {{will}} I eat…?
                -source-id melb
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o {{ba ku}} lo cmalu noltru cu junri te pinka 
                -english {{Then}} the little prince said, earnestly,
                -source-id litt
                -note This sentence is in the "narrative tense," in which the reference point is the time within the story itself. Thus, {{ba ku}} is taken to be the future relative to the events described in the previous sentences, thus its translation of "then". Note that the {{ku}} cannot be elided in this sentence, or the {{ba}} will take {{lo cmalu noltru}} as its reference point.
            -example:
                -lojban doi ga'i nai noltru mi'a pu jinvi lo du'u do {{ba}} se catra
                -english Oh, your Majesty, we thought you {{would}} be killed!
                -source-id wond
                -note This is an example of a {{ba}} inside a subordinate clause that expresses a tense <i>relative to the main clause</i>. Since the main clause is in the past tense, the {{ba}} creates the effect of "future-in-the-past." The event expressed by the subordinate clause is still in the past, though later in time than the event expressed by the main clause, according to the context.
        -meaning:
            -context When followed by a sumti 
            -exp Specifies the reference point to be the attached sumti. Essentially means "after ...". 
            -example:
                -lojban .i ro da {{ba}} lo nu .a bu limna lo lalxu cu simlu lo ka ba'o cenba
                -english Everything seemed to have changed {{since}} her swim in the pool.
                -source-id alic
        -meaning:
            -variation selba
            -context {{se ~}}, when followed by a sumti 
            -exp Specifies the time distance to the reference point to be the attached sumti.
            -note Using {{se}} with {{ba}} isn't permitted in CLL Lojban, but is permitted in camxes beta, camxes experimental and zantufa.
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi kansa {{seba lo mentu be li ji'i reno}}
                -english I {{will}} be with [you] {{in about twenty minutes}}.
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker Lucilla

-entry:
    -word bo
    -gloss Grouping: short scope link, strong binding
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho BO
        -meaning: 
            -exp Short scope link. Used in various grammatical constructions and acts as a high precedence link, overriding usual left-grouping rules. Chains linked with {{bo}} are grouped to the right. Specific usages are explained below.
        -meaning:
            -context Inside a tanru 
            -exp Joins tanru units, binding them together closely.
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o ze mo'o fenki tcati {{bo}} kansi'u
                -english VII. The mad tea{{-}}party
                -source-id alic
                -note The {{bo}} directly corresponds to the hyphen in the English text. Without the {{bo}} the tanru will group as {{[fenki tcati] kansi'u}}, which roughly translates to "the <em>mad-tea</em> party".
        -meaning:
            -context Directly after a connective 
            -exp Override the default left-grouping in chained connectives. Constituents connected by connectives with {{bo}} are grouped first. Chains linked with {{bo}} are grouped to the right.
            -example:
                -lojban .i ku'i mi pu ku tcidu ba'e so'u pagbu be le to'e censa se ciska gi'e ba bo ga'orgau le cukta {{gi'e bo}} co'a bevri
                -english Yet I had read only the least fragment of that blasphemous rune before closing the book and bringing it away.
                -source-id book
                -note The English sentence is grouped as "I had read... before [closing the book and bringing it away]". If the underlined {{bo}} wasn't there, it'll group as "I had [read... before closing the book] and bringing it away". This example also shows the right-grouping rule with chained {{bo}}: The {{gi'e ba bo}} in the example has a lower priority because it is to the left of the underlined {{gi'e bo}}.
        -meaning:
            -variation CLL
            -context Between a NAhE and a sumti 
            -exp Binds NAhE and the sumti together.
            -note Not required in later grammars such as camxes beta or zantufa.
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi ka'e binxo lo makcu prenu noi se cinri no {{na'e bo}} lo namcu
                -english I may become like the grown-ups who are interested in anything nothing {{other than}} figures.
                -source-id litt
        -meaning:
            -context After {{.i}} and a BAI modal 
            -exp Forms a sentence link along with {{.i}} and the BAI modal. The resulting sentence link ({{.i BAI bo}}) tags the latter bridi as a whole by the BAI modal, and incorporates it to the former (main) bridi. {{BRIDI<sub>1</sub> .i BAI bo BRIDI<sub>2</sub>}} has a similar meaning to {{BRIDI<sub>1</sub> BAI lo su'u BRIDI<sub>2</sub>}} or {{lo su'u BRIDI<sub>2</sub> cu SELBRI lo su'u BRIDI<sub>1</sub>}} (where {{BAI}} is the modal for {{SELBRI}}), but {{.i BAI bo}} also asserts that both the linked bridi are true.
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o lo ka jinga nalka'e cu nalvai {{.i mu'i bo}} mi klama be tu mutce bo djica
                -english It doesn't matter that I can't win, {{because}} I really want to go there.
                -source-id aisl
                -note The second {{bo}} in this example is of another usage (that inside a tanru). 
        -meaning:
            -context After {{.i}} and a tense 
            -exp Forms a sentence link along with {{.i}} and the tense. The resulting sentence link ({{.i TENSE bo}}) tags the latter bridi as a whole by the <strong><em>opposite</em></strong> of the tense, and incorporates it to the former (main) bridi. {{BRIDI<sub>1</sub> .i TENSE bo BRIDI<sub>2</sub>}} has a similar meaning to {{BRIDI<sub>2</sub> TENSE lo su'u BRIDI<sub>1</sub>}}, but {{.i TENSE bo}} also asserts that both the linked bridi are true.
            -note The implication of the relationship between connected sentences is the opposite for modals and tenses. The reason is likely historical, as said in <i>Lojban for Beginners (p. 110)</i>: "The very astute reader will have noted that ‘afterwards’ should have been {{.i pu bo}}; the analogy with {{ba ku}} won out, though."
            -example:
                -lojban .e'u mi'o klama lo korbi {{.i ba bo}} mi do jungau lo lisri be mi
                -english Let us get to the shore, {{and then}} I'll let you know my story.
                -source-id alic
        -meaning:
            -context After a connective and a BAI modal 
            -exp Binds the connective and the model so that the model signifies the relation between the connected elements. {{.i je BAI bo}} are similar in meaning to {{.i BAI bo}}, and the meanings of other connectives can inferred analogously.
            -note This usage is not unique to {{bo}}. Other grouping words like {{ke}} can also do this, but only those with {{bo}} are attested. 
            -example:
                -lojban .i ca ro dunra lo clika co'a dunja {{je ja'e bo}} sligu
                -english Every winter the moss freezes {{and becomes}} solid {{as a result}}.
                -source-id anta
        -meaning:
            -context After a connective and a tense 
            -exp Binds the connective and the tense so that the tense signifies the time relation between the connected elements. {{.i je TENSE bo}} are similar in meaning to {{.i TENSE bo}}, and the meanings of other connectives can inferred analogously.
            -note This usage is not unique to {{bo}}. Other grouping words like {{ke}} can also do this, but only those with {{bo}} are attested.  
            -example:
                -lojban .i loi sorska cinta ko fi ko'a cu se punji {{.i je ba bo}} loi xekri tabno ko'a gacri
                -english Paint it with colorful pigments, {{and soon enough}} it'll be covered in black charcoal.
                -source-id arma
        -meaning:
            -context After plain {{.i}}
            -variation zantufa
            -exp Forms a sentence link along with {{.i}}. The resulting sentence link separates bridi, but doesn't reset bridi level. Multiple bridi separated with {{.i bo}} shares the same prenex.
            -note The above explanation is taken from roljbogu'e chatlog, under said explanation {{.i bo}} is analogous to {{.i je}}. In actual usage, however, {{.i bo}} often resets bridi level, making {{.i ji'a}} a closer analogue.

When elidable, {{cu}} is usually elided when only pro-sumti precede it; when the preceding sumti is more complex (for example, {{la'e di'u}} or quotes), {{cu}} is sometimes kept to keep the sentence structure clear. 
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo pixra {{cu}} mapti
                -english One drawing goes along all right.
                -source-id litt
            -example:
                -lojban .i do lo nu djuno lo du'u ma kau smuvanbi {{cu}} nitcu lo nu jdice lo du'u ma kau smuni
                -english You'll [often] need to know the context to decide what things mean.
                -source-id tokp
                -note An example of {{cu}} closing off multiple subordinate clauses ({{lo nu djuno ...}} and {{lo du'u ma kau smuvanbi}}) at once. In this sentence, the full terminator sequence in place of {{cu}} would be {{vau kei ku vau kei ku}}. Also note that there can be a {{cu}} in {{ma kau (cu) smuvanbi}}, but it's elided: as there aren't terminators needed in the first place, using {{cu}} here offers no real benefit.
            -example:
                -lojban .i se cau le cartu poi dunda ke'a mi {{cu}} bajra litru se ka'a le balvi poi mi cuxna ku
                -english So without the map that was given to me, [I'll] run towards the future that I've chosen myself.
                -source-id rout
                -note An example of elidable {{cu}} kept for clarity. The last {{ku}} in this sentence is kept for metrical purpose.
        -meaning:
            -exp Starts a bridi tail (main selbri + trailing sumti if any). Termintate with {{vau}}.
            -note This sense is compatible with the above sense, and also enables {{cu}} to appear when there are no preceding sumti. This sense of {{cu}} is the basis of JACU and is supported by camxes beta, camxes experimental and zantufa.
            -example:
                -lojban .i sei fa'a ti'a catlu {{cu}} zvati fa pa nobli je ro'opno simlu zdani
                -english They looked back. There they saw a single, elegant Western-style home.
                -source-id rest
                -note As a {{sei}}-structure isn't a sumti, there are no sumti before {{cu}}, so this sentence doesn't parse in CLL/BPFK Lojban. In CLL/BPFK Lojban, {{se'u}} must be used in place of {{cu}} here to close off the {{sei}}-structure properly. 
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi dansu je {{cu}} kansa la terdi lo ka carna
                -english I dance and spin with the Earth.
                -source-id zaho
                -note An example of JACU. In this sentence, {{je}} connects two bridi-tails, {{dansu}} and {{cu kansa ...}}. Replace {{je cu}} with {{gi'e}} to convert it to CLL/BPFK Lojban.

-entry:
    -word do'u
    -gloss Elidable Terminator: terminates vocative phrase
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho DOhU
        -meaning:
            -exp Ends vocative phrase, which is started with {{DOI}} or {{COI}}. Elidable.
            -note For demonstration purposes, the example sentences here only shows {{do'u}} in unelidable position. See the words in selma'o {{DOI}} and {{COI}} to see elided {{do'u}}.
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{ju'i vitke do'u}} sutra klama .e'o
                -english {{Hey [visitors]!}} Come here quickly!
                -source-id rest
                -note If the {{do'u}} was elided in this sentence, the {{vitke}} would run into the {{sutra klama}} behind and form a tanru.
                -noshownote Original has {{vitka}}, which I presume is a typo of {{vitke}}.
            -example:
                -lojban .i .o'u {{nu'e do'u}} mi'o mo'u sepli simxu
                -english Don't worry &mdash; {{I promise}}, we'll be together again.
                -source-id dans
                -note If the {{do'u}} was elided in this sentence, the {{nu'e}} would take {{mi'o}} as a component of the vocative phrase, making {{mi'o}} the intended listener instead of the x<sub>1</sub> of the main selbri.
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{doi .dinas. noi dirba mi do'u}} .au do mi kansa lo nu vi cnita
                -english {{Dinah, my dear!}} I wish you were down here with me! 
                -source-id alic
                -note If the {{do'u}} was elided in this sentence, the vocative phrase would close right before the {{kansa}}.

-entry:
    -word doi
    -gloss Voicative: identifies the listener ("you")
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho DOI
        -meaning:
            -exp Generic vocative marker; identifies intended listener/audience. When followed by a sumti, the sumti is the intended listener/audience. When followed by a cmevla, it is understood as {{doi la CMEVLA}}. When followed by a selbri, it is understood as {{doi le SELBRI}}. {{doi}} opens a vocative phrase, which automatically closes when the sumti or selbri after {{doi}} is completed. The vocative phrase can also be closed explicitly with {{do'u}}.
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o {{doi mi poi mo'u verba}} .i pau ma poi prenu cu se prami
                -english {{To me when completing childhood}}, who do [you] love now?
                -source-id lett
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{doi dirba}} o'u dai ko sursipna iu cai
                -english {{Darling,}} (I feel you are relaxed), have a restful sleep <3
                -source-id lenr
                -note An example of {{doi SELBRI}}.
            -example:
                -lojban .i {{doi .dinas. noi dirba mi do'u}} .au do mi kansa lo nu vi cnita
                -english {{Dinah, my dear!}} I wish you were down here with me! 
                -source-id alic
                -note An example of {{doi}} followed by a cmevla. A cmevla inside a vocative phrase can take a relative clause/phrase, and is taken as the same as {{doi <em>la</em> CMEVLA &lt;relative phrase/clause&gt;}}.

-entry:
    -word e
    -gloss Connective: sumti afterthought and
    -type cmavo
    -definition:
        -selmaho A
        -meaning:
            -exp Logical connective: sumti afterthought and. Connects two sumti, such that the containing bridi is true if and only if it is true for each of the connected sumti. 
            -note Supplanted with {{je}} in JACU.
            -example:
                -lojban .i po'o do {{.e}} mi zvati
                -english Just you {{and}} me [are here].
                -source-id eggq
            -example:
                -lojban .i .ei mi pajni ji'u lo se zukte {{.e nai}} lo valsi
                -english I ought to have judged by deeds {{and not}} by words.
                -source-id litt
        -meaning:
            -context ~ {{bu}} 
            -exp Letteral for e. When used alone, refers to the most recent sumti starting with {{e}}.
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o {{la .em.}} ca lo nu klama ti te zu'e lo nu bu'u xabju cu citno je melbi speni .i lo solri jo'u lo brife cu cnegau {{.e bu}} ji'a
                -english When {{Aunt Em}} came there to live she was a young, pretty wife. The sun and wind had changed {{her}} (= Aunt Em), too.
                -source-id wond
    -definition:
        -selmaho I
        -meaning:
            -variation ajbo
            -exp Equivalent to {{.i ji'a}}. Starts a sentence that is a direct continuation of the last sentence. Sentences separated with {{.e}} are considered to refer to the same event/concept, and are only seperated becuase of grammatical reasons.
            -note Adopting ajbo prerequires adopting JACU. {{.e nai}} is neither defined or used.
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o ca le prula'i mi tolcirko lo tutci pe po skami xrazba {{.e}} mi finti le'i tolmulno pixra be lo skapypre .i ai ba di'a gunka tu'a ti
                -english (New topic) Last [time] I recovered my digital painting tools {{and}} I made this unfinished drawing of a skinwalker. I intend to keep working on this. &lt;picture&gt;
                -source-id rolj
                -speaker la srasu
                -note This utterance is in a divergent variety that has many differences with the baseline grammar: <ul><li>{{po}} is used as in jboponei, where it converts a bridi directly to a sumti, and corresponds to {{lo su'u}} in baseline.</li><li>{{.e}} is used as in ajbo, where it continues the same concept with another sentence, and corresponds to {{.i ji'a}} in baseline.</li><li>{{le'i}} is used as in gadganzu, where it functions as a demonstrative article (think of it as pointing to the &lt;picture&gt;), and corresponds to {{ti noi ... (ku'o)}} in baseline.</li></ul> This utterance serves as an example of the modularity of Lojban variaties, as said three differences from the baseline are unrelated to each other and can be employed independently.
