
-entry:
    -word pagbu
    -gloss part, component &mdash; whole, to have ... as its part 
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 is a part/component/piece/portion/segment of x_2 [where x_2 is a whole/mass]; x_2 is partly x_1.
            -example:
                -lojban .i do mi {{pagbu}} tai lo nu lo pelji cu {{pagbu}} lo cukta
                -english You {{are a part of}} me, like paper {{is part of}} a book.
                -source-id pram
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o zoi ty. e .ty cmavo fi lo du'u lo lifri cu sepli {{le drata pagbu be le jufra}}
                -english The particle <i>e</i>, which [introduces] a direct object, separates it from {{the rest of the sentence}}.
                -source-id tokp
                -note The grammatical term "direct object" is translated as {{lo lifri}}, lit. "experiencer".
            -example:
                -lojban .i do {{se pagbu}} ro da poi do djuno ja lifri ke'a ca lo do pu nunji'e
                -english You {{have within you}} all the knowledge and experiences of all your past lives.
                -source-id eggq
