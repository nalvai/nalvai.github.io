
-entry:
    -word pendo
    -gloss friend, to befrind, friendly &mdash; to have friends
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 is/acts as a friend of/to x_2 (experiencer); x_2 befriends x_1.
            -example:
                -lojban .i na ku ro prenu cu {{se pendo da}}
                -english Not every one has {{had a friend}}. 
                -source-id litt
            -example:
                -lojban .i je .o'e nai {{no pendo}} cu kansa do
                -english And {{none of [your] friends}} are by your side.
                -source-id colo
            -example:
                -lojban .i mi lu'ei co'o sai do li'au {{lo pu co'u pendo be mi}}
                -english I said "Farewell." to {{my no-longer friend}}.
                -source-id skab
                -note {{lu'ei ... li'au}} encloses a grammatical Lojban text and turns it into a selbri meaning "x<sub>1</sub> says &lt;text&gt; to x<sub>2</sub> with expressive medium x<sub>3</sub>".

    -word pensi
    -gloss to think of ..., to think about ..., thinker &mdash; to be thought of, to be thought about, (a) thought
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 thinks/considers/cogitates/reasons/is pensive about/reflects upon subject/concept x_2.
            -note x<sub>2</sub> can be either concrete or abstract.
            -example:
                -lojban .i je'u do se {{pensi}} mi .i mi {{pensi}} ca ro vanci do
                -english Truly, I {{think about}} you, I {{think about}} you every evening.
                -source-id pram
            -example:
                -lojban mi {{pensi}} lo'e nu zerle'a lo remna .e nai lo pixra
                -english I {{was thinking of}} the spiriting away of human beings rather than pictures.
                -source-id disa
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o ku'i ny. catlu mi gi'e spuda {{lo mi se pensi}}
                -english But he looked at me steadily, and replied to {{my thought}}.
                -source-id litt

-entry:
    -word preti
    -gloss question, (of a question) to be about &mdash; subject of question, to be ask about with (a question) &mdash; to ask a question about ... &mdash; to be asked a question about ... 
    -type gismu
    -definition:
        -meaning:
            -exp x_1 (quoted text) is a question/query about subject x_2 by questioner x_3 to audience x_4.
            -example:
                -lojban .i lu lo se citka ku mo li'u {{preti fi}} la melbi la melbi
                -english "What happened to the stuff I was going to eat?" Melbi {{asked}} themself.
                -source-id melb
            -example:
                -lojban ni'o lo ca tcini be lo bangu cu ka'e jai se pajni ta'i lo nu dafyfa'i {{so'o ralju preti be lo by cecmu nunpli}}
                -english The current status of a language can be assessed by answering {{a few key questions about community language use}}.
                -source-id asse
            -example:
                -lojban .i py. ca lo nu do py. tavla lo cnino pendo cu no roi {{te preti lo vajrai}}
                -english When you tell them about [your] new friend, they never {{ask [you] any questions about essential matters}}.
                -source-id litt
            -example:
                -lojban .i lo tedyskepre cu {{te preti}} fo lo tumfakli'u gi'e rejgau fi lo ri se morji
                -english The geographer {{asks}} the explorers {{questions}}, and he notes down what they recall.
                -source-id litt
